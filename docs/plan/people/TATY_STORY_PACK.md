## 1. You, in the game

### Querida Taty [Dear Taty]

You got off the bus one stop early "for the warm-up". By the time you reached the gate, a stray cat had adopted you, and four years later the dawn run club had forty members and the cat was still in charge.

That's you in {{GAME_NAME}}. You're a visiting sports-science student from Brazil who runs the 5:30 a.m. run club and the Friday games night, and you are never, ever tired. You tease people while you lap them. You hug them after you beat them. You're competitive, not rude, and you're the first one there when somebody is hurt, stuck, or hasn't come out of their room for a week.

The game follows a fresher (the player) through five years of queues, exams, power cuts, a strike, football and friendship. Whenever something needs carrying faster than is humanly possible (torches, water, diesel, messages, a footballer's lucky sock), you're the one running it. Over five years you become the fresher's coach, Jega's warm-up and rehab partner, Dr. Zainab's co-founder of the Campus Cat Committee, and the person who teaches a whole campus that "one more lap" is always a lie.

And underneath all the running, your story has a quiet turn in it. The girl who is never tired learns, a little at a time, that sometimes the bravest pace is slow, and that sitting still with someone counts as showing up. You still win games night, though. Obviously.

We think it's a lovely part. We've written it for your voice. Now please tell us everything we got wrong, and which Portuguese words you'd actually say.

### The rules that protect you

These are written into how the game is built, not just promised.

- **You approve everything.** Every line, every outfit, your cartoon likeness, Pixel's name and look, before release and again whenever we add anything new.
- **Your voice is only ever yours.** Your recordings are used for your character and nobody else's. They are never used to train or make an AI copy of your voice, and never edited to say something you didn't say.
- **The kill switch.** If you ever want out, the owner switches you off for every player at once. Every mission you're in still works without you (each one has a fallback), so you're never trapped in the story.
- **Never romance.** Your character is never in a romance, never flirted with, never the subject of a "will they, won't they".
- **Never carries the plot.** You help with one step, use your power, say a few lines. The story never depends on you.
- **Never sees the mystery.** The campus has a long, strange secret. You're never shown it and never asked to explain it. (Pixel sometimes stares at things. Nobody explains why. That's as close as you get.)
- **Modest, and kind.** Always modestly dressed; the camera never lingers. The jokes are about situations (laps, garri, power cuts, a cat who steals things), never about anyone's tribe, faith, body or money.

## 2. Your arrival

*This is the first thing anyone sees of you. If someone plays as you, it opens the game. If they play as the fresher, it plays the first time they meet you. About 25 seconds.*

**Scene: dawn, the main road into {{TOWN}}.**

A long-distance bus pulls up at a roadside stop, a kilometre short of the university. The door hisses. Taty hops down in running kit with a backpack on, waves to the driver, and starts jogging.

- **DRIVER**: "Madam! The university is the next stop!" **(new)**
- **TATY** (jogging backwards, waving): "I know! Warm-up! Obrigada! [Thank you!]" **(new)**

Pink sky, dust, a goat watching from a wall. After a hundred metres, a small scruffy tabby with one folded ear falls in beside her and matches her pace exactly.

- **TATY**: "Oi. [Hi.] …You're fast. Okay. Okay! Who's your coach?" **(new)**

At a junction, a trader with a table of everything (padlocks, phone chargers, a single cat carrier) watches them come. Taty doesn't stop running. She jogs on the spot, pays, and holds the carrier open. The cat considers it. Then she gets in, as if she'd booked it.

- **TATY**: "Nossa. [Wow.] You've done this before." **(new)**

The university gate, first light. Taty arrives jogging, a backpack and a cat carrier. She sets the carrier down to catch her breath (she doesn't need to; it's manners). The door swings open by itself. The cat steps out, looks at the campus, and walks in first, tail up, as if she owns it.

- **TATY** (laughing): "Bora! [Let's go!] She's already decided." *(from the story plan)*
- **TATY** (calling after her): "Okay, you're Pixel! Is Pixel okay? Pixel, wait for me!" **(new)**

She jogs in after the cat.

### Your arc, in one breath

Year 1, you outrun everything: goats, the dark, the squad, an okada. Year 2, your cat becomes famous, and on one long night at the hospital you find out what "tired" means and don't hide it. Year 3, in the strike, you meet the thing running can't fix, and you learn to walk half a lap with someone who can't manage a whole one. Year 4, you teach the fastest boy on campus that slow is how you get fast twice. Year 5, you run the far end of the longest night, and then, at convocation, you choose to walk slowly. You never once stopped being competitive. You just learned there was more than one way to win.

## 4. Your signature moment: the cat chase

*Mission 37 (Year 2): Scout in the Stands. The stadium, the Cup semi-final. This is the scene people will clip and share. Here it is in full, with every line.*

### Before: an hour to kick-off

A professional football academy has sent a scout to watch Jega. And Jega can't play: his two lucky socks have vanished from the washing line behind the security post. The fresher has chased the thief across three districts. It was the goat. The goat ate one. Sergeant Bilkisu has taken a statement from the goat.

So Jega plays in one lucky sock and one of Helen's. You're on the Education bench with the water bottles. Pixel is on the bench beside you, very still, looking at nothing. Nobody thinks anything of it. That's their first mistake.

### The first half

Jega is brilliant. Four thousand people in the stand. The scout writes things down. You're on your feet for most of it.

- **(new)** "Jega! LEFT! The other left! Your left! Okay, any left!"

At half-time Jega sits down on the bench, peels off the precious recovered sock to fix a blister, and lays it, very carefully, on the bench beside him.

Pixel looks at the sock.

## 7. How to record (the short version)

You don't need a studio. Your phone is fine.

1. **Find a soft, quiet room** (curtains and a bed: good; bathroom or kitchen: echo). Turn off fans and the air conditioning.
2. **Hold the phone a hand's width away,** slightly to one side so your breath doesn't pop.
3. **Say the code, then the line,** like this: "M-zero-three-seven, mid-two, t-zero-one… *PIXEL. That is a man's entire career!*" That's how we match your file to the scene.
4. **Do two or three takes** of anything you like, each a little different: louder, cheekier, faster. We'll pick.
5. **Change the words,** especially the Portuguese. If a line doesn't sound like you, say it your way and note it. Your version wins.
6. **Short sessions are best:** thirty to forty minutes, water nearby. (Yes, even for you.) Send the files however suits you.

The full voice script (every line, with a mood and a cue for each) comes as its own PDF. **At the end of that PDF is a one-page volunteer agreement** in plain English: what we may use your recordings for, that they're never used for an AI copy of your voice, how you're credited, and how to withdraw. Please read it, sign it, and send a photo of it back with your first recordings. If anything in it is unclear, ask before you sign.
