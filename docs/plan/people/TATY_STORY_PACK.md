## 1. Dear Taty

We are making a game about five years of university life in northern Nigeria, and we would love you to be in it: as yourself, with your own voice, your own energy and a superpower that could only be yours. Also a cat. The cat is not optional.

This pack shows you exactly what we have in mind: who you would be, every mission you would appear in, the lines we have drafted for you, and what we would ask of you. Nothing goes into the game until you have read it and said yes, and you can change or refuse any line.

## 2. What the game is (one page, no spoilers)

You arrive as a fresher at a big fictional federal university. On your first morning you chase a runaway goat through the food court; at sunset there is a note under your door that says somebody is watching.

Over five in-game years you go to lectures and sit exams, survive hostel life and "sapa" (being broke), cook garri when the power goes, play football, swim in the dam, train in the gym, fall in and out of love, and slowly uncover a mystery that runs under the whole campus: why the lights keep failing, who the quiet man in the old lab coat is, and why the campus cats sit in a ring in one garden every evening.

It is an open world with a real calendar: weekdays feel like school, weekends like freedom, exam weeks like panic. The tone is warm and very funny. Nobody dies, nothing is crude, and we laugh *with* people, never at them. The look is a fun, expressive cartoon style.

There is one story, the same for every player. But players can unlock **special characters**, real people who appear on campus as friends and can also be played. You would be one of the first.

## 3. Who you are in the game

**Taty: never tired, usually winning, always with a cat.**

- **Your role on campus.** A visiting sports-science student who runs the dawn run club and Friday games night. A stray cat adopted you on your first day and now rides in your hood. We have called her **Pixel**; rename her if you like.
- **Personality.** Warm, cheeky and competitive. You tease people while you lap them, and you are the first one there when somebody is hurt or stuck. You hug people after you beat them.
- **Your look (cartoon style, from your photos).** Long straight black hair; a black choker. Four outfits to start, all modest: gym wear for the run club; a floral dress with white sneakers for campus days; a black romantic dress for dinners; and a rock-concert look for games night. When you win, you throw the horns sign.
- **What you love.** Cats and games. Both are in your missions.
- **Your voice.** Yours. We write the lines; you say them your way. You told us "Portuguese or Italian-looking": tell us which few home words you would actually say ("Bora!" is our placeholder) and we will use those.
- **Your superpower: Never Tired.** Infinite stamina. Sprinting, swimming, dancing and football never drain you, and food matters less. A stray cat follows you everywhere.

## 4. How the player meets you

In the very first ten minutes, the fresher is chasing a goat through the food court. You jog through the scene the other way, a cat in your hood, not breaking stride:

> "Bora! Run, fresher, the goat is winning! Don't look at me, I'm on lap six!"

They meet you properly a few weeks later at football trials, where you take the coach's whistle and run the warm-up backwards, chatting, lapping the whole squad twice.

## 5. Your signature moment: the cat chase

**Mission 37, "Scout in the Stands" (Year 2), the stadium.**
It is the Cup semi-final. A professional scout is in the stand to watch Jega, the campus football star, who cannot play without his two lucky socks. A goat has eaten one. And Pixel, who has been sitting quietly on the bench, picks up the other one in her mouth and runs.

She goes the full length of the pitch, between both teams, during play. You go after her. You vault the advertising boards, slide under the corner flag, run the whole touchline and back without slowing. Both teams stop. Four thousand people start chanting "Pixel! Pixel!" The player runs it with you, and only keeps up by staying close.

You catch her on the centre spot, hold her up like a trophy, and throw the horns.

Draft lines (all changeable):
- "PIXEL. That is not yours. That is a man's entire career."
- "I'm not tired! I'm never tired! I'm just very, very angry!"
- (vaulting the boards) "Sorry! Sorry! Cat! Sorry!"
- (to the referee) "Don't blow that. If you blow that, she runs again."
- (holding her up) "She says sorry. She doesn't mean it. She's a cat."
- (to Jega, handing over the sock) "It's a bit wet. Left boot first. Go."

## 7. Your own missions, in full

These three are yours. The player can do them with you even before unlocking you.

**T1 · One More Lap** (Year 1 onward, 05:30; the ring road).
Your dawn run club. The player must keep up for one lap; you talk the whole way and are never out of breath. Pixel rides in your hood.
- "Bora! One more lap."
- (after it) "I lied. Two."
- "You're not dying. I've seen dying. This is just Tuesday."
*Reward for the player:* a hidden stamina treasure at the far bend; the run club as a daily activity.

**T2 · Cat Committee** (Year 2 onward, dusk; the Engineering Garden and the hospital).
You and Dr. Zainab, a young doctor who also loves cats, have founded the Campus Cat Committee and conscripted the player. Count the cats, name the unnamed (the player chooses; the names stay in their save), and build a feeding station from workshop scrap. The cats will not leave their ring in the garden at dusk, and they all turn their heads at once to watch somebody the player cannot see.
- "The cat likes you. I'm still deciding."
- Dr. Zainab: "Wash your hands. Did I stutter?"
- "They do this every evening. I asked the old man in the coat why. He said, 'They are better at this than I am.'"
*Reward:* a cat follows the player for a week.

**T3 · Games Night** (Year 3 onward, Fridays 20:00; the Student Union).
Your Friday games night, in the rock look: three rounds of whatever you pick (a board game, the dance machine, arm wrestling, which you win laughing). The player has to beat you at one.
- "I'm competitive, not rude. I'll hug you after I destroy you."
- (winning) "YES!" (the horns) "Sorry. Not sorry. Again?"
- (losing) "Meu Deus. Okay. Okay! Rematch. Now. Don't go home."
*Reward:* a cat-print bag (extra inventory space); your friendship.

## 9. Your unlock

You get a **personal code**. Enter it and you play the whole game as yourself: your look, your voice on the player's lines, your power, your cat, and your own reactions in shared scenes. The code is yours alone: it is checked on our server, it can be changed if it ever leaks, and nobody else can use it.

Later, only if you agree, other players could unlock your character through an event or a purchase, with a share of the revenue going to you. That is a separate decision and a separate agreement; saying yes to this pack does not commit you to it.

**Your voice is yours.** Your recordings are used only for your character. They are never used for any other character, never used to train or generate a synthetic voice, and never altered to say things you did not record.

## 10. What we would need from you

1. **Your yes** to the character as described, or your changes.
2. **Line approval.** We send your script (about 200–300 short player lines plus the scenes above); you mark what to keep, change or cut.
3. **Reference photos** for an "inspired-by" cartoon likeness: one clear front view, one side view, and a few outfits. The result is a stylised cartoon that people will recognise as you, not a photo copy. You approve it before anyone sees it. The photos stay private and are deleted on request.
4. **Voice recording, later.** On your phone is fine; we send a simple guide. Two or three short sessions.
5. **A short written agreement** (below), signed by both of us.

## 11. Consent and permissions: your rights, in plain words

- **You approve** your character's look, every outfit and every line before release, and again whenever we add new material.
- **You can change** anything at any time: a line, an outfit, the cat's name, the way a scene treats you.
- **You can withdraw.** If you ask, we switch your character off for all players (the game is built so this can be done at once) and remove it from future versions within an agreed time.
- **Nothing embarrassing.** Your character is never used in romance, is always dressed modestly, is never made the butt of a joke about who you are or how you look, and is never placed in content you have not seen.
- **Where it appears:** in the game, and in its trailers and screenshots, only with your approval of each use.
- **Your data:** photos and raw recordings are kept private, out of the public game files, and deleted on request.
- **Money:** nothing is being sold at launch. If your character is ever sold, you receive an agreed share, in writing, first.
- **This page is a summary, not the contract.** The full agreement will say all of this precisely.
