# Building a game from zero: the social media series

**For:** the owner, to record and post himself. Captions, voiceover lines, what to show, and where to find the footage.
**Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts (vertical 9:16, 20–60 s). A longer YouTube video once a month (8–15 min) stitches the shorts together.
**Voice:** yours, or a character from the game as narrator. Captions always on. Face optional.

---

## 1. Set-up (do this before Day 0)

1. **Pick a handle and name** that match the game's working name, so it can change later. For example, "Building FSLG" or "[Your name] builds a game". Use the **same handle on every platform.**
2. **Bio:** "Building a Nigerian campus open-world game from zero 🎓🎮 | Day X | Play the demo ↓". Add the website or Discord link once it exists.
3. **Profile picture:** the game logo, or your MIB character.
4. **A folder system for footage:**
   - `before/`: old versions;
   - `bugs/`: funny glitches;
   - `wins/`;
   - `decisions/`;
   - `voice/`.

   The coordinator already keeps every agent screenshot in `~/Downloads/FSLG_agent_images/`, and the trailers are in `docs/trailer/`.
5. **A screen recorder:**
   - on a phone, the built-in one;
   - on a Mac, ⌘⇧5;
   - record gameplay at 60 fps where possible.
6. **A simple editor:** CapCut or similar, for captions, zooms and sound. Check its current terms for commercial use.
7. **Posting rhythm:** 3–5 shorts a week, at the same times (evenings Nigerian time often do well; test it).
8. **Rules for yourself:**
   - be honest about using AI tools (label it where the platform asks);
   - don't fake progress;
   - reply to comments;
   - never post other people's faces or voices without permission.

---

## 2. The story arc (why people will follow)

The series is not "look at my game". It is **"watch a Nigerian guy try to build the game he wishes existed, with AI helpers, and make it good enough for the world."** The bad early versions are the best part: people love an underdog glow-up. Every episode ends with a question or a cliffhanger.

**Running gags:**
- "The AI said it was fixed. It was not fixed."
- The sapa meter for the project budget.
- "Day X: the characters still look like sticks."
- Bolaji's head through the car roof.

**Recurring segments:**
- **"Glow-up Friday":** before and after.
- **"Bug of the week":** the funniest glitch.
- **"You decide":** polls (name, character, feature).
- **"Meet the cast":** each character, later with the real voice.

## 3. The episodes

Each entry has:
- **Hook:** the first 2 seconds, on screen and spoken.
- **Show:** the footage.
- **Say:** the voiceover.
- **End:** the call to action.

The material for episodes 1–12 already exists (the 2D era is described from memory; screenshots of it may not exist).

### Day 0: "I'm building the game I wish existed"
- **Hook:** "There's no game about Nigerian campus life. So I'm making one."
- **Show:**
  - quick cuts of the current best footage (the trailer: docs/trailer/FSLG_trailer.mp4, the extended trailer, and the opening preview);
  - then black;
  - then text: "But it didn't start like this…"
- **Say:** "Five years on campus: sapa, lectures, friends, love, the hostel wahala. I wanted to play that. Nobody made it. So I'm building it from zero, and I'm taking you with me."
- **End:** "Follow to see how bad it was on day one 😭"

### Day 1: "The first version was… 2D"
- **Hook:** "This is what the AI gave me when I said 'make me an open-world game'."
- **Show:**
  - any early 2D or flat screenshot you have;
  - if none survive, re-enact it by describing it over a black screen with a funny drawing;
  - text: "2D. Flat. Sad."
- **Say:** "I asked for GTA on a Nigerian campus. I got… a map from a primary school exam. I said no. We start again."
- **End:** "Should I have quit here? 👇"

### Day 2: "Making it 3D: the campus is born"
- **Hook:** "From flat to 3D in one day."
- **Show:**
  - the first 3D campus shots (blocky buildings, trees);
  - a fly-over (the opening film aerials can work as "later" contrast).
- **Say:** "The buildings were boxes. The trees were green balls. But I could WALK. It felt like magic."
- **End:** "Guess which campus it's inspired by 👀"

### Day 3: "The map was bad"
- **Hook:** "My map was so bad I got lost in my own game."
- **Show:**
  - the early map vs the zoomable map with waypoints, search and legend;
  - the minimap tap opening the full map.
- **Say:** "First map? Useless. Now: zoom, search, waypoints, a legend. Small win."
- **End:** "What should every campus map have?"

### Day 4: "The characters look like sticks"
- **Hook:** "Why does my character walk like he owes someone money?"
- **Show:**
  - the early stiff walk;
  - then the animation pass (anim_v2 contact sheets: sitting, falls, car doors, hands on the wheel).
- **Say:** "Walking, running, falling, getting up, getting into a car, opening the door. Every one had to be taught. The AI kept saying fixed. It was not fixed. Until it was."
- **End:** "Next: the cars 🚗"

### Day 5: "My cars were sinking into the road"
- **Hook:** "Bug of the week: the car is swimming in the road."
- **Show:**
  - the old wobble or sink clip if you have it;
  - then the crash sheets: car hits bicycle, okada, wall (car_bike_sheet.jpg, car_okada.jpg, car_wall.jpg);
  - the keke with 3 passengers, and the okada with 2 behind.
- **Say:** "Cars sank. Bikes wobbled. The bicycle crash sounded like a pot falling. Now crashes have weight: a bus beats a car, a car throws a bicycle, and you fall off and get up."
- **End:** "Should getting hit by a keke be funny or serious?"

### Day 6: "Heads through the roof"
- **Hook:** "My driver's head was outside the car. The whole time."
- **Show:** the before frame (head poking through the roof), then the after (seats, interior, steering wheel turning).
- **Say:** "Nobody noticed for days. Now every vehicle has real seats, and a test fails if any head touches the roof."
- **End:** "Glow-up Friday is coming 🔥"

### Day 7: "The trailer: with my own voice"
- **Hook:** "I recorded the trailer voice in my room."
- **Show:**
  - FSLG_trailer.mp4 (the 56 s version with your voice);
  - a quick behind-the-scenes of the waveform (vo_waveform.png).
- **Say:** "I didn't want an AI voice. So I recorded it myself. One take. Okay, five takes."
- **End:** "Want to voice a character? Comment 🎙️"

### Day 8: "The opening scene: my parents dropping me off"
- **Hook:** "Every Nigerian student remembers this day."
- **Show:**
  - opening-film frames: the car through the gate, the bags, the porter, the hug, the parents driving away;
  - the old version's awkward hug (holding hands) vs the fixed one, if you have both.
- **Say:** "Mum: 'Eat. Read. Pray. Call me. In that order, but call me first.' The first version of the hug looked like a handshake. We fixed it."
- **End:** "What did YOUR parents say on drop-off day?"

### Day 9: "The AI picked the wrong roommate"
- **Hook:** "I chose Bature as my roommate. Sadiq was in my room."
- **Show:** the room choice, then the bug, then the fix and test list.
- **Say:** "Small bug, big lesson: if the game lies to you once, you stop trusting it. Now a test checks every roommate, every time."
- **End:** "Worst roommate story? 👇"

### Day 10: "I gave my friends roles in the game"
- **Hook:** "My real friends are now characters in my game."
- **Show:**
  - the cast list;
  - the voice role PDFs (blur the private parts);
  - friends recording lines (with their permission).
- **Say:** "Jega, the football guy. Shamsu, 'Professor', who explains everything with big words. Bolaji, tough outside, soft inside."
- **End:** "Which one are you in your friend group?"

### Day 11: "The phone, like GTA"
- **Hook:** "Your character pulls out his phone, and someone snatches it."
- **Show:**
  - the phone (p_sheet.png: home screen, texts from Mum, friends);
  - the phone-snatch chase if captured.
- **Say:** "Texts from Mum. Request money from home, and sometimes she refuses with a lecture. Use your phone in a crowd at night? Someone might run with it."
- **End:** "Mum or Dad: who sends money faster?"

### Day 12: "Swimming at the dam"
- **Hook:** "I added a dam you can swim in. With fish."
- **Show:** the dam shots (FSLG_agent_images/dam/): fish, the visitor centre aquarium, diving, the breath meter.
- **Say:** "Tilapia, catfish, Nile perch. A visitor centre with an aquarium wall. Swim too long underwater? You float up. Nobody drowns in this game."
- **End:** "What should be hidden at the bottom of the dam?"

### Day 13: "The big decision: rebuilding everything"
- **Hook:** "I'm about to throw away the engine I spent weeks on."
- **Show:** the best current footage, then a slow zoom on the blocky characters, then text: "It's not good enough."
- **Say:**
  - "The story is good. The world is big. But the characters look like sticks, and I want people to look real.
  - So we're moving the whole game to a proper game engine, on its own website. I kept a copy of the old version, just in case."
- **End:** "Am I crazy? Be honest."

### Day 14: "Engine battle: Babylon vs PlayCanvas"
- **Hook:** "Two game engines. Same scene. Only one survives."
- **Show:** the bake-off screenshots side by side (from Stage 2), and the FPS numbers on a cheap phone.
- **Say:** "Same character, same car, same garden. Which looks better? Which runs on a ₦60k phone?"
- **End:** "Vote: left or right?" (Then reveal the decision in the next post.)

### Days 15–30: "The glow-up month" (one per day or every other day)
- First realistic character render ("He has a FACE now").
- Weight and muscle demo ("Too much suya → 😂").
- The first rebuilt street, before and after.
- The first stat treasure found.
- A friend records a voice line for their character.
- Bug of the week (new-engine bugs are funny too).
- "You decide": the star lead (Jega, Nusaiba, Shamsu, Mubarak or Zainab).
- The opening scene rebuilt with real faces (Glow-up Friday).
- The website goes live: "You can play it now."

### Monthly long video (YouTube, 8–15 min)
**"I built a Nigerian campus open-world game: month 1"**, in five chapters:
1. Why.
2. The terrible start.
3. What went right.
4. What went wrong (bugs, usage limits, internet dropping).
5. What's next.

Use the shorts as chapters.

## 5. Captions and hooks bank

**Hooks:**
- "POV: you're building a game with AI and it keeps lying."
- "Day X of making a Nigerian GTA, but it's school."
- "The AI said fixed 🙂"
- "This bug cost me 3 days."
- "Rate my glow-up 1–10."
- "Nobody asked, but I added suya."

**Endings:**
- "Follow for day X+1."
- "Comment your campus and I'll add a reference."
- "Which character should be playable?"

**Hashtags:** use a small, consistent set (for example #gamedev #indiegame #nigeria #naijagames #campuslife #buildinpublic), and check what's trending in your niche weekly.

---

## 6. Growth rules
1. Post consistently, even when progress is small. Small honest progress beats long silence.
2. Reply to the first comments quickly.
3. Turn comments into features ("you asked for X, here it is"), and credit the commenter.
4. Do one "you decide" poll a week.
5. Once there's a demo, every post points to it.
6. When influencers join (section 9 of the master guide), co-post the reveal.
7. Keep a simple sheet: date, post, views, follows, comments, and what worked.
