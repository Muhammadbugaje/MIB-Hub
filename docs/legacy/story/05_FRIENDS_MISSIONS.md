# Freshers School Life — Friends Missions (co-op, 2–6 players)

Twenty-five missions for players in the same room on the same Wi-Fi. They are separate from the story:
they never set story flags, anyone can host from the phone's Multiplayer tab, and every one can be
finished in 4–10 minutes. Read with `02_BIBLE.md` (tone, banned list).

## How they work

- **Host and join.** One player hosts with a room code. The host picks a mission or "Shuffle".
- **Scaling.** Each design says how it changes from 2 to 6 players. Where teams are needed and numbers
  are odd, the smaller team gets a stated bonus.
- **Winning.** Every mission has a clear win condition and a result screen with three awards. One award
  is always for something kind or funny (most assists, best dressed, most honest loser).
- **No sore losers.** Nobody is eliminated for more than 20 seconds. Knocked-out players come back.
- **Variety.** Each mission draws from lists (routes, hiding places, weather, time of day, vehicles), so
  two sessions are not the same. "Mutators" are optional twists the host can switch on.
- **Rewards.** Cash and XP for everyone, a little more for winners, and co-op-only cosmetics (team
  scarves, a six-seat keke skin, a "Class of Friends" shirt from the MIB Store).
- **Fair play.** Cheats and the hidden character are off unless the host turns them on for everyone.

## Mutators (host options, any mission)

| Mutator | Effect |
|---|---|
| Harmattan | Dust haze; shorter sight |
| They have taken light | Night, lamps off, torches only |
| Garri week | Everyone starts hungry; food stalls give boosts |
| Market day | Roads crowded with walkers and carts |
| One ride | Everyone uses the same vehicle type |
| Swap | Every 60 seconds, two random players swap positions |
| Tiny purse | Prize money is paid in ₦5 coins scattered at the finish |

## The missions

### Racing

| # | Name | What you do | Players and scaling | Win | What changes each time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Ring Road Rumble | Lap race round the ring road on a vehicle class the host picks. | 2–6, free for all. | First over the line after 2–4 laps. | Vehicle class, direction, weather, three shortcut gates that open at random |
| F2 | Keke Relay | Teams share one keke. Each player drives one leg and must tag in at a shuttle stop. | 2–6 in two teams (1v1 to 3v3); the smaller team's keke is 8% faster. | First team to finish all legs. | Leg order, stops used, one "passenger" leg where you must carry a walker gently |
| F3 | Okada Sprint Cup | Three short sprints between landmarks; points per finish. | 2–6. | Most points after three sprints. | Start and finish pairs drawn from twelve landmarks |
| F4 | Bicycle Derby | Bicycles only; sprinting drains energy, so pacing matters. Food stalls on the route refill. | 2–6. | First to finish; ties broken by energy left. | Route, stall positions, a hill section |
| F5 | The Long Road | Point-to-point from the Main Gate to Phase 2, any vehicle, traffic on. | 2–6. | First to the shuttle park. Crashing into walkers adds 5 seconds each. | Traffic density, time of day, roadworks |
| F6 | Skate Line | Skateboards down the boulevard collecting trick gates. | 2–6. | Highest score in 90 seconds. | Gate layout; a "follow the leader" round |

## Session shapes

So an evening does not repeat, the host can pick a ready-made set:

- **Quick three** (15 minutes): one race, one hunt, one co-op, chosen at random.
- **Sports day** (25 minutes): F17, F18, F16, F19 with a medal table.
- **Night shift** (20 minutes): F21, F22, F23 with "they have taken light" on.
- **Grand tour** (40 minutes): six missions, one from each group, a running league table, and a trophy
  scene at the Selfie Garden for the winner and a wooden spoon (a real spoon, for garri) for last place.

## Notes for building

- All 25 reuse systems the story already has: races, flight, kola nuts, jobs, the rhythm game, the
  arcade, sparring, shades, convoys.
- Position updates travel through the existing room relay. Missions are designed to tolerate about a
  third of a second of delay: no mission needs frame-exact contact between players except F16, where
  hits are judged on the attacker's screen and knock-downs are announced, not disputed.
- Every mission ends on a shared result screen with a group pose, because the photograph is the reward.
