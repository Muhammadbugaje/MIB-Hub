# Freshers School Life — Characters and Backstories

Private to the owner. Revision 4 (full sheets for every character). This file is the authority for every person in the story; where
`02_BIBLE.md` section 8 differs, this file wins. All names are first names only. Except for the owner's
own place as the maker of the game, every personality, job, history and line is invented; none
describes a real person.

**How to read a sheet.** Voice and speech; looks (skin, hair, build) and outfits (a default and two
alternates — characters change clothes with the day, the season and the occasion); want, flaw and
secret, with the scene that reveals the secret; the five-year arc; ties; one thing they love that has
nothing to do with the plot; a quirk; whether the player can court them (opposite sex only); gifts
(three loved +8, three liked +3, three hated −2 — a gift only scores if the player heard them mention it
earlier, and the "heard in" note says where); and the missions they appear in.

**Decisions taken in this revision (the writer's calls, flagged for the owner):**

1. **Bolaji is male, and he is "Big Sly".** The owner's arc for Bolaji (bully → ally → barber → stops the
   next fresher tax) is the arc already written for the court boss Big Sly, so they are one person:
   Bolaji is his name, "Big Sly" his court name. For the builders: the game's NPC records `sly` and
   `bola` become one character; mission m16's two separate talk steps become Bolaji and Abbas.
2. **Firdausi is Bature's cousin** (not Sadiq's). She and Bature are the default best friends for female
   and male players.
3. **Roommates.** The player chooses a room at the start (see section 1). Bature is the default male
   roommate and the companion on most missions; Sadiq becomes a course mate for male players who do not
   pick him.
4. **Tunde the artist** is Tunde Aro, not the playable Tunde Bakare.
5. **Lula Ajaka is the man and Faty Ajaka the woman** of the funniest couple on campus.
6. **The Kanti Sports shopkeeper** in m81 is renamed Shuaibu, so the name Muhammad belongs only to the
   gentle stranger.

## 1. Choosing a room

At the start the player picks one of five rooms (men's or women's hall) or "Let fate decide". The
roommate changes the opening scenes, the daily lines in the room, one friendship subplot, and who the
first best-friend candidate is. Every roommate also lives in the world as a friend.

| Room (men) | Roommate | Benefit | Drawback | Flavour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ribadu 2-14 | **Bature** (default) | Comes on most missions; his jokes calm angry people | Eats your garri | "Riddle me this: why is your garri lighter?" |
| Suleiman 1-07 | **Sadiq** | You always wake on time; he cooks | Quiet; you must start the talking | Half his money goes home |
| Akenzua 3-02 | **Muktar** | Room is organised; a committee forms for every problem | Meetings. So many meetings | His girlfriend Summy chairs his committees |
| Alex 2-21 | **Yusuf** | Doors open for you (charm) | People believe him more than you | The girls' hostel knows the room number |
| Dan Fodio B-11 | **Khalid** | Carries anything; never late | Agrees too easily; vanishes for a semester in Year 3 | A notebook of favours he never calls in |

| Room (women) | Roommate | Benefit | Drawback | Flavour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Queen Amina 1-09 | **Firdausi** (default) | Knows everybody; best-friend scenes start early | Tells Bature everything | Bature's cousin |
| Queen Amina 2-03 | **Nusaybah** | Notices what others miss; study bonus | Bossy before breakfast | Engineer and psychologist in one room |
| Queen Amina 3-12 | **Aleeya** | Outfits and invitations | People assume you're her assistant | Secretly the funniest person you'll meet |

| Queen Amina 1-16 | **Lily** | Calm room; plants everywhere | Too gentle to wake you | Named after a flower; wrote to the Maker once |

Token in scripts: `@roomie` (default Bature or Firdausi). Where a line only suits one roommate, the
script gives variants.

### 1. Bature (bature) — roommate, best friend for male players, the default companion
- **Voice:** quick, bright, a laugh under every sentence; Hausa flavour. **Speech:** opens with "Riddle
  me this…"; ends arguments with a riddle that turns out to be the answer. Some call him "Berti".
- **Looks:** light-brown skin, low cut with a part shaved in, lanky, an oversized jersey and slides.
- **Outfits:** default — oversized jersey and slides; alternates — a tie-dye kaftan for Fridays; a borrowed blazer, sleeves too short, for the dinner.
- **Want:** to be taken seriously once. **Flaw:** hides every feeling behind a joke.
  **Secret:** he is top of the class in two courses and sits at the back so nobody expects it.
  Revealed in friendship scene 5 ("Bature's results", Year 4, m97 aftermath) and it changes m111: he
  answers the external examiner's hardest question for you.
- **Arc:** Y1 the clown who eats your garri → Y2 the one who makes the heist plan funny enough to work →
  Y3 the only one who keeps the room loud in the strike → Y4 his secret lands when the twins post the
  results list → Y5 he gives the class toast, and nobody laughs, because it is beautiful.
- **Ties:** Firdausi's cousin; trades riddles with Tanko (comic twin to Tanko's deep ones); argues with
  Tanko about the horse's name.
- **Loves:** collecting bottle caps by colour. **Quirk:** counts stairs out loud and always gets a
  different number.
- **Romance:** yes, for female players.
- **Gifts:** loved — groundnuts in a twist of paper, a riddle book, a jersey of the losing team (heard in
  m37, m40, m43). Liked — garri, zobo, a new bottle cap. Hated — anything "for reading", ties, silence.
- **Missions:** 40+ (see `STORYLINE.md`), s1, many ambient lines.

## 5. The mystery

### 82. The Maker (maker)
- **Voice:** quiet, exact, dry; later poetic and grand. Never a joke, never a fighter.
- **Looks:** light skin, dark coily hair, a lab coat older than you. **Outfits:** the lab coat over a

- **Mysteries kept open:**
  1. **His sister.** One line in Y1 (m11, at the pier: "My sister used to feed the fish here"), one photo in Y4 (a girl on
     the pier, face turned away), one almost-name in Y5 (he starts "Ai—" and stops). Never resolved.
     The Sculpture Garden has a statue of a girl feeding fish; nobody knows who carved it.
  2. **His absences.** Two to five in-game days at a time, without warning (flag `maker_away`):
     blackouts rise, shades thicken, one mission goes wrong. He returns, things improve. Never explained.
  3. **The second name** stitched inside his lab coat, found in Y4 (m99). Never explained.
  4. **The seventh plaque.** Blank until m116; afterwards it shows the player's name, and beneath it an
     older name scratched away.
  5. **The letter.** Chancellor Null's letter is in his handwriting (m115): he once accepted one.
  6. **The laugh.** Only Lula and Faty Ajaka have ever seen him laugh. They never say at what.
  7. **The bench.** The stone bench in the Sculpture Garden is warm even in harmattan. He never sits on
     it; he sits beside it.
- **Lines:** short and dry before the ring closes; after it, the 44 sayings (bible §18). Ten show-off
  "big word" lines and his Rumi lines (public-domain translations) are in `QUOTES.md`.
- **Gifts:** he accepts only one thing, once a year: a glass of water from the Dam, brought by hand.
