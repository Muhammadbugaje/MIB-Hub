# Freshers School Life — The Storyline

Private to the owner. Revision 4: Year 1 rewritten from the first moment (`scripts/Y1.md`, `scripts/Y1b.md`). The whole 120-mission story, beginning to end, with the new cast
and beats from the owner's latest specification folded into the arc approved earlier. Full scripts
are in `scripts/`; people are in `CHARACTERS_BACKSTORY.md`; quotations in `QUOTES.md`; jokes in
`../HUMOUR.md`.

## The story on one page

The game opens on registration day. You get off the bus at the Main Gate with one bag, one admission
letter and your mother's list of instructions. Baba Porter shows you five rooms and the five people in
them; you pick one (or "let fate decide" and get Bature, or Firdausi for a woman). A soft-spoken
stranger called Muhammad tells you not to take the shortcut, and Malam Garba signs your first file.
You know nobody. Five years later you leave in a gown, and half the school knows your name.

The light keeps going off, and something moves in the dark. Students call it NEPA. The Maker, a quiet
man with coily hair who keeps a Tesla coil in the Engineering Garden, calls it **Entropy**: everything
runs down unless somebody puts energy in.

Long ago a professor and six students planned a ring of **six coils** to keep the school's light
steady. Each built a part alone, the parts did not fit, five went home, and one stayed to keep the
single working coil alive. He never graduated. He is the Maker. He has a sister nobody can explain,
he sometimes vanishes for days (and the dark gets worse), and there is another name sewn inside his
coat. None of that is ever explained.

Each year Entropy finds a new way in, and each year you, your friends and, in the end, the whole school
find and light one more coil. Every coil lit makes the campus visibly richer: new colours on the
buildings, more vehicles, more people, brighter nights. On the last night the Maker hands back a
letter he once accepted, the ring closes, and he walks out of the garden a free man. The last mission
is a gentle stranger called Muhammad, at the gate, asking you the way to the Senate.

**The message, never preached: light is something people do together.**

## The world gets better: prosperity

Each district has a tier from 0 to 5. Every coil lit raises every district one tier; a few missions
raise one district on their own (the lamps on the Phase 2 road; the buka saved; the mall made honest).

| Tier | What you see |
|---|---|
| 0 | Year 1 (m1–m35): some walls unpainted, dim lamps, few vehicles, sparse crowds; the first coil at m36 raises every district to tier 1 |
| 1 | Fresh paint on the main buildings; more walkers; a second shuttle |
| 2 | Market stalls with awnings; murals at the court; the second keke and a second-hand saloon for sale |
| 3 | Lit paths everywhere; benches and planters; sports car and plane upgrade available; more side missions |
| 4 | Buildings in their final colours; fountains on; night markets; brighter music layers |
| 5 | After m116: every lamp on, festival lighting, the Maker walking free, full crowds |

Workshops let you help build: wood, sand, cement and parts from the mechanical, electrical and civil
workshops (Foremen Danjuma, Ikenna and Asabe) go into new vehicles (the second keke, a restored
saloon) and buildings (the repair clinic, the skate park). **The wooden cliff tower** above the Dam is
started in Year 3 ("we ran out of wood"), revisited in Year 4, and left unfinished on purpose, for
future missions.

## The Maker's absences

Two or three times a year, without warning, the Maker is not in the garden for two to five in-game
days (flag `maker_away`). Blackouts come more often, shades are thicker, one mission goes wrong in a
small way (a race starts in the dark; a lamp you built fails), and nobody can say where he went. Then
he is back, says nothing about it, and things improve. Aunty Asiya worries; Tanko says "Which?" when
asked if he knows. Never explained. Scheduled absences: m27–m30 in Year 1 (the coil goes dark, the eel lashes out, the
levy works the dark market; he is back at m30, unexplained), then m41–m42, m63, m83, m109; plus random
ones in free roam.

## Year 1 — Arriving (m1–m36)

**Theme.** Can a stranger belong here?
**Thread.** The light keeps going, and in the dark something moves. A missing fuse, a logbook with six
circles on page forty, a man in an old lab coat by a bench he never sits on, an eel under the Dam, and
a caped face on the Senate steps that the freshers themselves invented. Written fresh in revision 4;
full scripts in `scripts/Y1.md` (m1–m18) and `scripts/Y1b.md` (m19–m36).
