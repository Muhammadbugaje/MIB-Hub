# Year 4 — Image (400 level): scripts m79–m99

Revision 3: m86 is now Hock Day (the time loop) and m87 is Heist 3; other new beats are lettered.

Format and rules are in `../02_BIBLE.md`, sections 14–19. Every spoken line ends with an empty voice
id `[v: ]`. `@place` is the industrial-training placement chosen in m79 (mall plant room, dam station,
airfield, radio, or Works); `@boss` is its supervisor. A fame meter (NEW) runs through this year.

### m79 — IT letter
- Semester 1 · Gate 81 · Shape: collect, choice · Giver: Mrs. Adebayo · Thread: D
- Places: Senate, @dept, Bank · Time: one long day
- Needs: m78 · Music: `motif_work` as a comic march

**Premise.** One letter, four stamps, three offices, two "come back tomorrow"s and a bank queue. Then
a choice of where you train for six months.

**Beats**
1. [talk: adebayo] Start at the registry.
   - MRS. ADEBAYO: Where is your photocopy? Of the form you have not yet collected? Exactly. [v: ]
2. [collect: stamp ×4] Department, bursary, bank teller, registry again.
   - MR. CLEMENT: Network. [v: ]
   - MR. CLEMENT: …It has come. Quickly, before it goes. [v: ]
3. [turn] At the bank, the queue is forty people and one is a goat.
   - MALAM GARBA: Patience, my child. The one who queues well, eats well. [v: ]
4. [talk: khadija] The placement list.
   - DR. KHADIJA: Five places. Choose where you will learn, not where you will be comfortable. [v: ]
5. [choice] Pick a placement.

**Expanded beats (rev 4).**
x1. [walk: bature] Bature joins the queue "for moral support" and immediately eats your snack.
   - BATURE: Riddle. What has four stamps and still cannot fly? Your IT letter. [v: ]
   - BATURE: Don't laugh. It is a deep riddle. It is about Nigeria. [v: ]
x2. [talk: kb] KB's shop is on the way. A photocopy costs ₦50 today. Yesterday it was ₦30.
   - KB: Prices are up because the mood is up. Something is coming. I can feel it in the paper. [v: ]
   - YOU (option): KB, is that a warning? [v: ]
   - KB: It is a photocopy. ₦50. Or ₦45 if you smile. [v: ]
x3. [turn] The bursary officer's network is down. So is the generator. So is the officer.
   - MR. CLEMENT: Come back after lunch. [v: ]
   - BATURE: Sir, it is after lunch. [v: ]
   - MR. CLEMENT: Then come back after the next lunch. [v: ]
x4. [switch: Bature] Bature takes the bank queue while you run for the last stamp (switch roles).
   - BATURE: I am holding your place. If the goat moves, I will move. We are brothers now. [v: ]
x5. [find] Behind the registry window, a half-built wooden tower in an old photograph.
   - MRS. ADEBAYO: That? The cliff tower. They ran out of wood in my first year. Still out. [v: ]
x6. [phone] A message from Tanko, unsigned, on your phone.
   - TANKO (text): The letter with four stamps opens one door. The fifth stamp is a person. [v: ]
x7. [talk: khadija] Dr. Khadija notices Bature's riddle notebook and reads one page.
   - DR. KHADIJA: Who wrote these? They are wrong in a way that is nearly right. [v: ]
   - BATURE: A friend. A very handsome friend. [v: ]
x8. [choice-callback] If you chose the dam station in m70's call, Baba Dam's neighbour waves already.
   - BABA DAM'S NEIGHBOUR: The quiet one's student. Good. Bring patience, not shoes. [v: ]
x9. [emote: laugh] On the way out, the goat from the queue is wearing your IT letter.
   - BATURE: He got placed before you. The goat has a future. [v: ]
   - WALKER: Is that goat on SIWES? [v: ]
x10. [sleep] Back at the hostel, Bature counts the stamps like money.
   - BATURE: One, two, three, four. Rich. Spiritually. Not financially. [v: ]

**Choice.** Mall plant room (boss: Engr. Aliyu if on probation, else a stern stranger) / Dam station
(Baba Dam's neighbour) / airfield (Captain Uche) / campus radio (DJ Kabiru) / Works. It changes the
site and boss lines in m80, m84 and m97.

**Sets / pays.** Sets `placement_<id>`.
**Around it.**

### m80 — First day at work
- Semester 1 · Gate 82 · Shape: routine (job) · Giver: @boss · Thread: D
- Places: @place, @hostel · Time: a working week
- Needs: m79 · Music: `motif_work`; a day-off tone

**Premise.** Your supervisor hands you a broom. Then a clipboard. Then, by Friday, a spanner. Fill the
logbook every day, or all of it on Friday night with a different pen.

**Beats**
1. [go: @place] Report at eight. (On a job: site overalls; "on a job" banner.)
   - @BOSS: IT student? Good. Sweep. Then we see who you are. [v: ]
2. [job: three tasks] Sweep, read six gauges onto a clipboard, fetch the right spanner from a wall of them.
3. [routine: logbook ×5] Each evening, write the day (one tap) or skip.
4. [turn] Friday: a real fault, and everyone senior is at lunch.
   - A COLLEAGUE: Don't touch it. …Do you know what it is? Then touch it. Carefully. [v: ]
5. [build: small fix] Tighten, reset, restart.
6. [talk: @boss] He reads the logbook.
   - @BOSS (kept daily): Five days, five pens' worth of truth. Good. Monday you get keys. [v: ]
   - @BOSS (filled Friday): Same ink. Same hand. Same hour. I was a student too. Do better. [v: ]

**Expanded beats (rev 4).**
x1. [routine: arrive 07:45] Be early on day one. The boss notices who comes first.
   - @BOSS: You are early. Good. Early people sweep. [v: ]
x2. [carry] The broom has a name written on it: "Property of whoever is new".
   - BATURE (phone): Send a photo. I want to frame your suffering. [v: ]
x3. [talk: abbas] Abbas is placed at the same site, in grease up to the elbows.
   - ABBAS: Don't call me John Carter here. These people will put it on a badge. [v: ]
   - BOLAJI (arriving): JOHN CARTER! The man of the hour! [v: ]
   - ABBAS: …I am going to unplug him. [v: ]
x4. [switch: Abbas] Hand Abbas the spanner while you hold the panel (switch to Abbas for the bolt).
   - ABBAS: Hold it straight. Straighter. That is crooked straight. Real straight. [v: ]
x5. [routine: logbook ×5] Each evening, fill the day's page. Skip a day and the pen colour changes.
   - BATURE: You used blue on Monday and black on Tuesday. Mr. Detective will catch you. [v: ]
x6. [turn] Friday: the boss checks every logbook in the room against the clock-in sheet.
   - @BOSS: Three of you were here at 07:45 on a public holiday. Interesting. [v: ]
x7. [choice] If your logbook is honest, nothing happens. If not, the boss smiles and says nothing.
   - @BOSS: I also wrote my logbook on Friday night. Once. Never again. Guess why. [v: ]
x8. [talk: kb] KB has started selling "logbook pens", one colour per weekday.
   - KB: Seven pens, ₦700. Or ₦500 for honest people. Are you honest? [v: ]
x9. [eat] Lunch: the site's canteen has rice, beans, and a lady who calls everyone "IT boy".
   - CANTEEN LADY: IT boy, eat. You are thin like a signature. [v: ]
x10. [sleep] Bature waits up with garri. He has eaten half.
   - BATURE: I tested it for poison. It is not poisoned. It is delicious. Sorry. [v: ]

**More beats (rev 4).**
y1. [talk: bature] Night shift: Bature brings a torch and a riddle for the long hours.
   - BATURE: What works all day and gets paid in experience? You. Welcome to IT. [v: ]
y2. [phone: maker_away] The garden lamps flicker on the way home. The Maker's bench is empty.
   - TUNDE (text): He's gone again. Two days. Everything hums wrong. [v: ]
y3. [emote: laugh] The site's goat (every site has a goat) eats your logbook's last page.
   - @BOSS: The goat has reviewed your work. It found it delicious. Rewrite it. [v: ]

**Rewards.** Weekly allowance, 1,300 XP, Department +3. Work shifts become repeatable.
**Sets / pays.** Sets `logbook_honest` or `logbook_friday`. Pays `aliyu_probation` if he is your boss.
**Around it.**
- PHONE (@roomie): You have a job with a uniform? Bring food from there. All jobs have food. [v: ]
- WALKER (a colleague): The last IT student broke the kettle. We don't speak of it. [v: ]
