# Ambient encounters e1–e31

Short "someone needs your help" moments, generated from these templates. Rules are in the bible,
section 12: a person calls out (never a marker), one objective, always declinable, a twist about one
time in five, people remember. Each template has three written variations (A, B, C). Every spoken line
ends with `[v: ]`. e31 (garri) was added after the owner's request.

Outcome key: **Help** what happens if you do it; **Decline** if you walk on; **Twist** the 1-in-5 version.
