# Opening film — arriving (plays once, before m1)

In-engine, letterboxed, skippable (tap SKIP or press Esc). Five acts: (1) aerial title over seven landmarks with captions; (2) the arrival below; (3) other freshers arriving; (4) a gameplay brief with caption cards; (5) the logo over the Main Gate, then control at the hostel. Narration is the owner's own trailer recording (static/audio/voice/intro/), the score is original (static/audio/fslg-intro/). Replay from Settings → "Replay the opening".
The room choice happens inside it, and the chosen roommate is the one who greets you. Old saves skip it.
Lines are spoken by MUM, DAD, BABA PORTER, the chosen roommate and the player. Every line ends `[v: ]`.

### m0 — Opening: arriving
- Before m1 · Shape: cinematic · Places: Main Gate, the porter's lodge, @hostel
- Music: `motif_school` (solo kalimba), then `place_hostel_morning`

**Premise.** Your family's car brings you through the Main Gate on registration morning. Your parents
help with the bags, the porter takes them to your room, you meet your roommate, and then they have to go.

**Beats**
1. [film] The family car rolls through the Main Gate in the harmattan haze and parks by the lodge.
   - DAD: There it is. The gate. I drove through this same gate thirty years ago. [v: ]
   - MUM: You drove through it on a bicycle. Don't start. [v: ]
2. [film] You climb out with Mum and Dad. Suitcase in your hand, bags in Dad's.
   - MUM: Hold the suitcase properly. It has your whole life in it. And my good pot. [v: ]
   - PLAYER: Mum, why is the pot in my suitcase? [v: ]
   - MUM: Because you will want soup at night, and I will not be here. [v: ]
3. [film] Baba Porter comes out of the lodge and takes the luggage.
   - BABA PORTER: Welcome, welcome. Give me that. Your mother packed this? I can tell. [v: ]
   - BABA PORTER: Come. Choose your room before the queue of mothers eats us. [v: ]
4. [choice: room] Choose your room. (Same board as m1 beat 3.)
5. [film] At the hostel the porter sets the bags down in your room. Your roommate is there.
   - BABA PORTER: Room delivered. Bags delivered. Child delivered. My work is finished. [v: ]
6. [film] You hug Mum, then Dad.
   - MUM: Eat. Read. Pray. Call me. In that order, but call me first. [v: ]
   - DAD: Don't follow bad friends. Follow good ones. Lead some. [v: ]
   - PLAYER: I will. Go before Mum cries. [v: ]
   - MUM: I'm not crying. It's the harmattan. [v: ]
7. [film] They drive away. You wave until the car is gone. Then you turn to your roommate.
   - DAD: Bye! Bring back the Tupperware! [v: ]
   - PLAYER: …Okay. I'm a student now. [v: ]
8. [film] The camera pulls back: other families unloading, hugs, suitcases dragged across the forecourt.
   - SENIOR: Fresher! Your mother is still at the gate! [v: ]
   - LOST FRESHER: Excuse me… which way is the Senate? [v: ]
   - SENIOR: Follow the orange building. You can't miss it. Everybody misses it. [v: ]
