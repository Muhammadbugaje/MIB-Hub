# Year 3 — Drift (300 level): scripts m58–m78

Revision 3: new cast and beats from `../STORYLINE.md` are added as extra beats (lettered a, b, c)
at the point shown. `[switch]` marks co-op swapping with no timer.

Format and rules are in `../02_BIBLE.md`, sections 14–19. Every spoken line ends with an empty voice
id `[v: ]`. This is the strike year: from m59 to m69 the campus is nearly empty, place music runs at
half its layers, and most people you meet are staff, traders and the few who stayed.

### m58 — Three hundred

- Places: @dept, Pepsi Garden, basketball court, @hostel · Time: day
- Needs: m57 · Music: `motif_work`, `motif_crew`

in Pepsi Garden, one not seen since Tuesday.

   - JEGA: I'm awake. I was reviewing. With my eyes. [v: ]

### m59 — Indefinite

- Needs: m58 · Music: `tension_low`, then place themes losing layers one by one; `motif_home`

### m60 — Those who stayed
- Semester 1 · Gate 58 · Shape: routine · Giver: Mai Shayi Audu · Thread: W
- Places: Tea stall, Main Gate, Mall, Sick Bay, Engineering Garden · Time: one slow day
- Needs: m59 · Music: place themes at half layers; `quiet` in the quad

**Premise.** Count who is left: a tea seller, a nurse, a porter, a man with a coil, and you. No
allowance is coming. Build a day, and a hustle, out of that. A slow day on purpose.

**Beats**
1. [talk: audu] Morning.
   - AUDU: Tea first. …There is nobody to talk about. Drink slowly. [v: ]
2. [routine: visit ×3] Greet Nurse Hauwa, Baba Porter and the Maker. Each asks one small thing.
   - NURSE HAUWA: You stayed. Good. Eat. Sleep. Please. And check on your roommate. [v: ]
   - THE MAKER: An empty school runs down faster. Come by. I like the noise you make. [v: ]
3. [turn] [bank] Your balance is not a number you can live on.
4. [choice: hustle] Pick two ways to earn. (Each starts a job: job vehicle and banner where it has one.)
   - EMEKA: Keke. Morning runs. I take thirty per cent, because I am fair. [v: ]
   - CHI-TECH: Hold this screwdriver. Don't touch anything else. Congratulations, you're a technician. [v: ]
5. [job ×2] Do one shift of each.
6. [eat: garri] Evening. It is garri week, and will be for a while.
   - @ROOMIE: Groundnut? In this economy? [v: ]
a. [déjà vu] At noon a walker passes and says, "Hot today, abi?" Five minutes later the same walker
   passes and says it again, same smile, same step. Only Wednesday, under her black umbrella, looks up.
   - WEDNESDAY: You saw that. Good. Most people don't. See you Wednesday. [v: ]
   - PLAYER: It is Wednesday. [v: ]
   - WEDNESDAY: Then see you next Wednesday. [v: ]
b. [find: notebook] Nusaybah has been tidying Khalid's corner and found a notebook.
   - NUSAYBAH: Wait. Look again. It's a list of favours. Every one he ever did. [v: ]
   - NUSAYBAH: Carried a bed. Fixed a tap. Walked Lily home. Hundreds. He never asked for one back. [v: ]
   - NUSAYBAH: People who keep lists like this are usually saving up to ask for something big. [v: ]
c. [sapa] Evening. The whole floor checks its balances together.
   - BATURE: I checked my balance and my phone apologised. [v: ]
   - FIRDAUSI: Sapa is not a phase. It is a lecturer. It takes attendance. [v: ]
   - @ROOMIE: I'm not broke. I'm pre-rich. [v: ]

**More beats**
1a. [talk: audu] Audu has started selling tea for ₦20 because he can't bear the empty bench.
   - AUDU: ₦20. Not for money. For company. You pay me with your sitting. [v: ]
2a. [talk: porter] Baba Porter has started sweeping corridors nobody walks in.
   - BABA PORTER: An empty hostel gets dusty faster. Like a heart. Don't tell anybody I said that. [v: ]
2b. [talk: kb] KB has written "STRIKE PRICES" on his board. Everything is cheaper. Except garri.
   - KB: Garri is up. Everything else is down. Read that slowly. It tells you who stayed. [v: ]
4a. [switch: Bature] On the keke shift, swap to Bature to haggle with a passenger who has no change.
   - BATURE: Madam, ₦100. You have ₦500? Then ride five times. It's a subscription. [v: ]
   - PASSENGER: …Fine. Take me round the fountain four more times. [v: ]
5a. [job: chi-tech] Chi-Tech fixes a phone by hitting it, then charges for "diagnosis".
   - CHI-TECH: Diagnosis: it needed a slap. Like all of us. ₦300. [v: ]
5b. [find] Under a repair bench, a phone with a cracked screen and a sticker: "Y.B." Chi-Tech grabs it.
   - CHI-TECH: That one's not for repair. It's been here eleven years. Somebody's paying rent on it. [v: ]
   - PLAYER: Who? [v: ]
   - CHI-TECH: Envelope. Every year. No name. Don't touch it. [v: ]
6a. [eat] A garri-week cookbook, read aloud by the roommate in the dark.
   - @ROOMIE: Monday: garri. Tuesday: soaked garri. Wednesday: garri, soaked, with hope. [v: ]
6b. [quiet] Before sleep, the coil's hum comes faintly through the window. It sounds tired.

**Choice.** Any two of: keke runs (Street), tutoring at Demonstration school (Books; leads to m61),
mall shifts (Vibes), phone repairs with Chi-Tech (Department). All are repeatable afterwards.
**Rewards.** Earnings, 1,000 XP. Unlocks m61.
**Sets / pays.** Sets `hustle_a`, `hustle_b`. Pays `vac_job`.
**Around it.**
- RADIO (Kabiru): Day nine. I have played every track twice. Send requests. Send anything. [v: ]
- WALKER (a trader): Students have gone. Even the goats look bored. [v: ]

### m61 — Small Sadiq
- Semester 1 · Gate 59 · Shape: quiz (teach mode, NEW) · Giver: Aminu · Thread: F
- Places: Demonstration Secondary School · Time: afternoon
- Needs: m60 · Music: `motif_crew` on a toy-piano tone

**Premise.** A boy at Demonstration school wants to be an engineer "like you". This time you ask the
questions, and choose how to explain when he gets one wrong.

**Beats**
1. [go: demo] A classroom with chalk dust and one pupil who has stayed behind.
   - AMINU: Are you the engineer? Uncle Sadiq said an engineer is coming. [v: ]
   - AMINU: Can you make light? Can you make a car? Can you make jollof? [v: ]
2. [teach: 5 questions] Pick a question, hear his answer, choose how to respond.
3. [turn] He gets one right that you got wrong in your last test.
   - AMINU: Is it not so? You said so yourself. Last time. Differently. [v: ]
4. [talk] He copies how you stand.
   - AMINU: When I come to your school, will you show me the Senate? [v: ]
   - PLAYER: I'll show you. [v: ]

**More beats**
1a. [look: classroom] The blackboard still has a lesson from before the strike: "PHOTOSYNTHESIS".
   - AMINU: Our teacher went home. I stayed. My uncle Sadiq says staying is a skill. [v: ]
2a. [teach] He asks a question back after every answer.
   - AMINU: Why? …But why? …And why is that? …Is there an end to why? [v: ]
   - PLAYER: Not really. [v: ]
   - AMINU: Good. I don't want an end. [v: ]
2b. [switch: Bature, optional] Swap to Bature to explain electricity with a riddle.
   - BATURE: Riddle me this: what runs without legs and lights without fire? [v: ]
   - AMINU: Electricity! [v: ]
   - BATURE: Correct. You're smarter than me. Don't tell anyone. Especially me. [v: ]
3a. [turn] He draws a circuit on the board, correctly, and you realise you'd drawn it wrong in Year 1.
   - AMINU: You put the battery backwards. My uncle showed me your notes. Last year. [v: ]
   - PLAYER: Your uncle showed you my notes? [v: ]
   - AMINU: He said they were good. Mostly. Except the battery. [v: ]
4a. [talk] He shows you his drawings: cars, mostly, and one of a lightning machine in a garden.
   - AMINU: There's a man with this machine. Everybody says he's a ghost. Is he a ghost? [v: ]
   - PLAYER: He's not a ghost. He drinks tea. [v: ]
   - AMINU: Ghosts can drink tea. I'm not convinced. [v: ]
4b. [emote: salute] He salutes you when you leave. You salute back. A teacher in the corridor laughs.
   - A TEACHER: He salutes the gate man too. And the goats. You're in good company. [v: ]
4c. [phone: sadiq] Sadiq, from home, sends a voice note.
   - SADIQ: He called me. He said "the engineer is real". Toh. Now he wants to be you. Sorry. [v: ]
4d. [walk back] Walking back past the gate, Muhammad is fixing a little girl's bicycle chain.
   - MUHAMMAD: Sorry. Excuse me. Almost done. [v: ]
   - PLAYER: You're not on strike? [v: ]
   - MUHAMMAD: Sorry. I'm not on anything. There. Ride slowly. [v: ]
   The girl rides off. When you look back, he's gone.
4e. [hostel] At home, you redraw your Year 1 circuit, correctly, and pin it above your bed.
   - BATURE: You were taught by a twelve-year-old today. How does it feel? [v: ]
   - PLAYER: Good, actually. [v: ]
   - BATURE: Riddle me this: who's the teacher when the student is right? …Both. Sleep. [v: ]
   - AMINU (text, late): Uncle says I must say thank you properly. Thank you properly. [v: ]
   - AMINU (text): Also, ghosts drink tea. I checked. I'm still not convinced. [v: ]

**If it goes wrong.** Impatient answers: he goes quiet; the lesson ends early with less reward and a
line from @roomie that evening ("He's small. Go easy.").
**Rewards.** ₦1,500, 1,000 XP, Books +3, Community +2. Tutoring becomes a repeatable hustle.
**Sets / pays.** Sets `met_aminu`. Plants m119 and m120.
**Around it.**
