# Year 5 — Leaving (500 level): scripts m100–m120

Revision 3: m103 is now Heist 2 (the Senate basement) and m107 is the dream mission; other new
beats are lettered.

Format and rules are in `../02_BIBLE.md`, sections 14–19. Every spoken line ends with an empty voice
id `[v: ]`. `@super` is the supervisor chosen in m101 (Dr. Okonkwo, Prof. Bello or Dr. Khadija).
m112–m116 are one long night and should be playable in one sitting, with a save after each.

- Needs: m99 · Music: Year 1's arrangements, fuller and slower; `motif_school`
