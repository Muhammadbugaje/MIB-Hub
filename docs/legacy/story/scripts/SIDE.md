# Side missions s1–s66

Optional, 8–20 minutes each. Each belongs to one person and makes them more themselves; the five per
named student double as their friendship scenes (scene 5 always reveals the secret and changes a later
mission). Format: header, cast, premise, beats with lines, choice, failure, outcome, and lines heard
around it. Every spoken line ends with `[v: ]`. "Act" is the year from which it is offered. Gifts
named in a beat are the ones the character mentions there, so they count as "heard" for the gift
system. Romance lines are written for the player's sex where it matters; nobody is ever won by
pestering, and "no" holds for a week.

---

### s1 — Sadiq's pot
Act 1 · collect · Giver: Sadiq · Hostel, market, Buka · Music: `motif_home`
**Cast.** Sadiq, Mama Nkechi, Hajiya Ladi, Bature (if roommate). **Friendship scene.** Sadiq 1 of 5.
**Premise.** His mother's soup needs six things from the market, and the pot nobody will admit to borrowing.
1. [talk: sadiq] He holds up his phone like it is a holy object.
   - SADIQ: My mother sent the recipe by voice note. It is eleven minutes. Listen with me. [v: ]
   - SADIQ: Minute four is just her greeting my uncle. Skip that. No, don't skip. Respect. [v: ]
   - BATURE: Riddle me this: what's eleven minutes long and has no pepper in it? This voice note. [v: ]
2. [buy ×6] Ugu, crayfish, stockfish, pepper, palm oil, seasoning at Hajiya Ladi's.
   - HAJIYA LADI: Stockfish for a student? Last price. …Fine. Student price. Don't tell anyone. [v: ]
   - HAJIYA LADI: Whose soup is this? His mother's? Then the pepper is free. Mothers are free. [v: ]
3. [find: pot] It is in Mama Nkechi's back room, with a note in Sadiq's handwriting: "Borrowed. Sorry."
   - MAMA NKECHI: I knew it was him. He returned it with soup inside. Take it. [v: ]
   - MAMA NKECHI: Tell him the pot has a name. Its name is "Bring Me Back". [v: ]
4. [cook: hostel cooker] Sadiq cooks; you stir when told and not before.
   - SADIQ: Stir. Not like that. Like you love it. Okay, like you tolerate it. Fine. [v: ]
   - SADIQ: She says "a pinch". My mother's pinch is a handful. We will use a handful. [v: ]
5. [eat] - SADIQ: It tastes like home. Small. Home, but small. [v: ]
   - SADIQ: Toh. Next week you buy the stockfish. That is how friendship works here. [v: ]
5a. [gift: sadiq] Give him the leftover crayfish. He loves anything from his mother's kitchen.
   - SADIQ: You listened. Nobody listens to minute four. You listened to minute four. [v: ]

**Choice.** Call his mother to say it worked (she cries, then asks if you have eaten) / let him call
alone (he tells you later she asked about "the friend who stirs badly").
**If it goes wrong.** Burn the soup: Sadiq eats it anyway, slowly, and says "It's home. Home burns too."
**Outcome.** Friendship Sadiq +6; soup is a food item for a week; "his mother's spices" heard.
**Around it.**
- WALKER: Who is cooking egusi in a boys' hostel? I'm coming with my plate. [v: ]
- BATURE: I didn't eat your soup. I tasted it eleven times. Like the voice note. [v: ]

- SADIQ: (a week later) The pot is back with Mama Nkechi. It has soup in it. Ours. [v: ]
