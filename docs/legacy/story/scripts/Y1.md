# Year 1 — Arriving (100 level): scripts m1–m18

Revision 4, written fresh from the first moment of the game. Year 1 second semester (m19–m36) is in
`Y1b.md`. Format and rules: `../02_BIBLE.md` §14–19; cast: `../CHARACTERS_BACKSTORY.md`; arc:
`../STORYLINE.md`; jokes: `../../HUMOUR.md`; quotations: `../QUOTES.md`. Every spoken line ends with an
empty voice id `[v: ]` and is ≤ 90 characters. Tokens: `@roomie` (chosen in m1; Bature or Firdausi by
default), `@hod`, `@hostel`, `@dept`, `@crush`, `@best`. **[switch]** marks a co-op beat where the player
can swap between themselves and the named companion; there is never a timer on a switch. Step types in
square brackets; NEW marks one the game does not have yet.

Year 1 keeps the shape the game already plays (registration day, the first blackout, the shades, the
court, the dam, the first races, Count Entropy, first flight) and rebuilds it around the new cast.
Facts Year 2 relies on, all set here or in `Y1b.md`: you know Malam Garba, Baba Porter, Mai Shayi Audu,
Dr. Okonkwo, Bolaji ("Big Sly"), Abbas, Kelechi, Baba Dam, Coach Danladi, Captain Uche, KB and the
Maker; the shades and the Count; the six coils premise; the first coil is lit at m36; the year ends
"Fresher no more" and "for tonight".

### m1 — Welcome, fresher
- Semester 1 · Gate 1 · Shape: escort · Giver: Baba Porter · Thread: D
- Places: Main Gate, @hostel, Senate, @dept · Time: early morning into noon · Weather: harmattan haze
- Needs: new game · Music: `motif_school` (solo kalimba), `place_hostel_morning`, `sting_friend`
- New things: room choice (NEW: `choice` step with portraits; see below), backpack, the phone

**Premise.** Registration day. You arrive with one bag, one admission letter and a mother's list of
instructions. Pick your room, meet the person you will share it with, and get your file signed before
the Senate closes.

**Why now.** It is the first moment of the game. Everything begins with a bed and a piece of paper.

**Beats**
1. [go: gate] Walk through the Main Gate with your bag.

   - CORPORAL PETER: Admission letter? Ehen. Welcome. Don't run. Don't fight. Don't fall in love. [v: ]

3. [choice: room] Choose your room. (NEW: a board of room cards. Each shows the roommate's name,
   portrait, one benefit, one drawback and one line of flavour. "Let fate decide" picks at random.)

### m2 — Belle first
- Semester 1 · Gate 1 · Shape: routine · Giver: @roomie · Thread: F
- Places: KB's shop, Mama Nkechi's buka, @hostel kitchen, your first lecture, @hostel
- Time: morning to night, day 2 · Weather: harmattan
- Needs: m1 · Music: `place_buka_day`, `place_class_hum`, `sting_friend`
- New things: eating, cooking at the hostel cooker (NEW: `cook` interaction, timer, bigger buff),
  garri, KB's shop, your first lecture

**Premise.** Day one of real life: eat, go to class, survive, come home. Simple. Except your money,
your roommate and the campus all have opinions.

**Why now.** You have a file and a timetable. Now you need a stomach and a routine.

**Beats**
1. [go: @hostelshop] Buy breakfast at KB's shop by the hostel gate.
   - KB: Welcome, new face! For your face today? ₦300 bread. [v: ]
   - PLAYER: Yesterday it was ₦250. [v: ]
   - KB: Yesterday your face was sad. Today you look like money. Prices follow faces. [v: ]
   - BATURE: KB, riddle me this: what gets more expensive the happier you look? [v: ]
   - KB: Your account. Bature, you owe me four hundred. Smile less. [v: ]
2. [buy: bread, garri] Buy bread now and a cup of garri for later. (Teaches buy and the belt slots.)
   - KB: Garri? Ah. First week and you're already buying garri. You're wise. Or broke. [v: ]
   - @ROOMIE: Garri is not food. It's a lifestyle with water. [v: ]
3. [eat] Eat on the spot before class. (Teaches eat: small buff.)
   - PLAYER: This bread is soft. Why is this bread so soft? [v: ]
   - BATURE: KB adds kindness. And something else. Don't ask what. [v: ]
4. [class] Go to your first lecture. Get two answers right. (Teaches the timetable strip and class.)
   NELT is full. A boy at the back is already asleep. A girl in the front row is drawing a diagram
   better than the lecturer's.
   - DR. OKONKWO: Good morning. I'm Dr. Okonkwo. If you're asleep, you're absent. [v: ]
   - DR. OKONKWO: Charge flows. Current is charge per second. Write that before you forget it. [v: ]
   - NUSAYBAH (front row, under her breath): She said "per second", not "per lecture". Wake him. [v: ]
5. [turn] [talk: @roomie] After class @roomie meets you outside. Your money is ₦1,200 lighter than you
   thought. Your bag has a hole. A small one. Exactly one bread wide.
   - PLAYER: My money. Where's my money? [v: ]
   - @ROOMIE: Check the hole in your bag. …Okay, it's a very honest hole. Everything fell out. [v: ]
   - BATURE (if roommate): Riddle me this: what has a mouth and eats your money? Your bag. [v: ]
6. [collect: coins ×4 at @hostelshop, @dept] Retrace your steps. Pick up your coins on the boulevard. (Teaches pick-up.)
   Each coin is beside a different person, and each one has something to say.
   - MARYAM (outside Page Turner Books): You dropped this. Also, do you want yam? I have yam. [v: ]
   - LYZZY (with a mirror): Don't move. Your face is a moment. Okay, you can move. Here. [v: ]
   - EMILY (out of breath): Is this yours? Strawberry? Sorry. Reflex. [v: ]
   - TUNDE (sketching on the steps, not looking up): Your mood is a wet blue today. Here. [v: ]
7. [go: @hostel kitchen] It's evening. Cook dinner at the hostel cooker instead of buying.
   (Teaches cooking: put rice and tomatoes in the pot, wait the short timer, eat for a bigger buff.)
   - SADIQ (passing the kitchen, or roommate): Cook it yourself, it's half the price. And twice the love. [v: ]
   - @ROOMIE: You're cooking? You? Should I call the Fire Station now or later? [v: ]
   - PLAYER: Later. Hand me the salt. [v: ]
8. [eat] Eat with @roomie on the corridor floor.
   - @ROOMIE: Tell me one thing about home. [v: ]
   - PLAYER: My little sister counts my shirts when I pack. She said I owe her two. [v: ]
   - @ROOMIE: Then she'll be here by Friday. Hide the shirts. [v: ]
9. [sleep] Go to bed. Day one: done.
   - BATURE (if roommate): Riddle me this: what goes up and down but never moves? Our fan. Night. [v: ]
   - FIRDAUSI (if roommate): The lizard says goodnight. I'm translating. Goodnight. [v: ]
   - MUKTAR (if roommate): Meeting adjourned. Minutes tomorrow. Goodnight. [v: ]

**Choice.** None. But what you cook first (rice, yam, or garri soaked with sugar) is remembered: in m16
@roomie brings "your dish" to the crew.

**If it goes wrong.** Fail the class: Dr. Okonkwo "marks you present but disappointed"; take any
other lecture that day. Burn the food (leave the cooker too long): eat it anyway, −5 mood, @roomie
laughs for a week. Never a dead end.

**Rewards.** ₦500 back from the coins, 400 XP, Academics +1, friendship @roomie +3, recipe: Jollof
(hostel). Unlocks m3.
**Sets / pays.** Sets `met_kb`, `met_maryam`, `met_lyzzy`, `met_emily`, `met_tunde`, `first_dish`.
Tunde's "wet blue" is the first of his colour lines; his sketch of you in m96 is dated this day.
**Around it.**
- RADIO (Kabiru): First lecture? Sit in the middle. Front row gets questions. Back gets sleep. [v: ]
- RADIO (Kabiru): KB says bread is ₦300 for happy people. Look sad, save fifty naira. [v: ]
- WALKER: I said "present" and I wasn't even in the room. I was outside. Present outside. [v: ]
- WALKER: Who is that girl drawing better than the lecturer? I'm scared of her. Respectfully. [v: ]
- PHONE (Mum): You cooked? You? Send picture. If it's black, don't send picture. [v: ]

### m3 — Two wheels
- Semester 1 · Gate 1 · Shape: delivery · Giver: Emeka · Thread: W
- Places: Main Gate, the bicycle stand, Page Turner Books, the Library
- Time: afternoon, day 3 · Weather: clear, windy
- Needs: m2 · Music: `place_gate_bustle`, `ride_light`
- New things: the bicycle, the Library, deliveries, gifts and listening

**Premise.** The campus is big and your legs are small. Emeka at the gate has a bicycle that needs a
rider and a box of books that needs to reach the Library before Mrs. Shehu closes the door.

**Why now.** You've walked enough to learn that you need wheels.

**Beats**
1. [talk: emeka] Meet Emeka at the Main Gate.
   - EMEKA: You! You look like someone with legs. Can you ride? [v: ]
   - PLAYER: I can ride. I've ridden. Once. In secondary school. Into a wall. [v: ]
   - EMEKA: Perfect, the wall is not on campus. Take this bicycle. Don't marry it. [v: ]
2. [ride: bike] Get on the bicycle. (Teaches E to mount, pedalling, the handbrake skid on X.)
   - BATURE: Riddle me this: what has two wheels, one rider and zero brakes? [v: ]
   - PLAYER: This bicycle? [v: ]
   - BATURE: This bicycle. Go slowly near the fountain. The fountain is undefeated. [v: ]
3. [go: pageturner] Collect the box of books from Maryam at Page Turner Books.
   - MARYAM: These are for the Library. Mrs. Shehu has asked for them since Ramadan. [v: ]
   - MARYAM: If you drop one, she will know. She knows the weight of every book. [v: ]
   - MARYAM: Do you want yam for the road? It's roasted. Take. Riding needs yam. [v: ]
4. [carry: books → library] Ride the books to the Library. (The box rides on the rack; bumps shed
   books. Pick up any that fall.)
   - LYZZY (from the path): We saw it first! You almost fell! Do it again, I wasn't filming. [v: ]
   - LILY (beside her, quietly): Don't. He'll actually do it again. [v: ]
5. [turn] At the fountain a goat steps out. You swerve. Three books slide into the water.
   A boy in a white shirt has already fished two out and is drying them on his sleeve.

   - PLAYER: Thank you! Who are you? [v: ]
   - MUHAMMAD: Someone who was walking past. Mind the goat. She doesn't apologise. [v: ]
6. [find: book] Fish the third book out of the fountain. (A short wade; −2 energy.)
   The book is a slim poetry collection. Someone has pressed a flower between two pages.
7. [go: library] Deliver the box to Mrs. Shehu before closing.
   - MRS. SHEHU: Twelve books. One is damp. One is very damp. One is a fountain. [v: ]
   - MRS. SHEHU: You tried. That is more than most. You may have a library card. Shh. [v: ]
   - BATURE: She said "shh" at the end. Is that a word or a warning? [v: ]
   - MRS. SHEHU: Both. Shh. [v: ]
8. [talk: emeka] Return the bicycle to Emeka. He tells you to keep it for the week.

   - EMEKA: Next week, buy your own. Or marry it. I said don't, but I can't stop you. [v: ]
9. [quiet] [talk: maryam] Tell Maryam the books arrived. Listen to what she says she wants.

   - MARYAM: Thank you. One day I want a book with my own name on the spine. Not anonymous. [v: ]
   - PLAYER: Not anonymous? [v: ]
   - MARYAM: Did I say that? I said "yam". Do you want yam? [v: ]

**Choice.** At beat 6: keep the pressed flower (it goes in your backpack; Lily asks about it in m16) or
leave it in the book (Mrs. Shehu notices and is quietly pleased: Library standing +1).

**If it goes wrong.** Drop more than half the books: Maryam re-packs them ("Again. Slowly. With yam.").
Late for the Library: Mrs. Shehu takes them through the window the next morning; the mission completes
with no card bonus.

**Rewards.** ₦800, 400 XP, bicycle (borrowed for a week), library card, a gift tip: Maryam loves a
notebook. Friendship Maryam +3. Unlocks m4.
**Sets / pays.** Sets `listened_maryam_book`, `flower_kept` or `flower_left`, `met_muhammad_2`. The
pressed flower pays in m16 (Lily) and is mentioned again in Lily's friendship scene 5.
**Around it.**
- RADIO (Kabiru): The fountain has claimed three cyclists this week. The fountain is winning. [v: ]
- RADIO (Kabiru): Library hours: until Mrs. Shehu says shh. Which is any minute. [v: ]
- WALKER: That goat has a name. Nobody knows it. She has a sense of humour. [v: ]
- WALKER: I saw a boy dry a book with his sleeve. Who raised him? I want his mother's number. [v: ]
- PHONE (@roomie): Are you alive? The radio said three cyclists. Count yourself. [v: ]

### m4 — They have taken light
- Semester 1 · Gate 2 · Shape: defend · Giver: @roomie · Thread: M
- Places: @hostel, the corridor, the courtyard, Senate · Time: 19:00 into night · Weather: still, hot
- Needs: m3 · Music: `place_hostel_night`, `tension_low` → `fight_shades`, `sting_reveal`
- New things: the blackout, the torch, the shades

**Premise.** The power goes off. Everyone shouts "Ah, they have taken light!" like every night. But
tonight, in the dark corridor, something moves that is not a student.

**Why now.** Three days in, you think you understand the campus. The campus disagrees.

**Beats**
1. [go: @hostel, night] Be in your hostel after dark.
   The whole floor is playing music from phones. Somebody is ironing. Somebody is singing badly.
   - BATURE: Riddle me this: what goes off every night and comes back like nothing happened? [v: ]
   - PLAYER: Light? [v: ]
   - BATURE: My cousin's boyfriend. But also light. Watch. Three, two… [v: ]
2. [turn] The power goes. The corridor screams the national anthem of the hostel.
   - CORRIDOR (everyone): Ah! They have taken light! [v: ]
   - @ROOMIE: Torch. Where's your torch? Your mother packed you a torch. Mothers always do. [v: ]
3. [find: torch] Find the torch in your backpack. (Teaches the torch, F.)
   - PLAYER: Found it. It's pink. It says "Mummy's Boy" on the side. [v: ]
   - @ROOMIE: It's a torch. It works. Nobody can read in the dark. [v: ]
4. [talk: @roomie] Something cold moves past your door. The fan stops before the light does.
   - @ROOMIE: Did you feel that? The air went the wrong way. [v: ]
   - NUSAYBAH (from the stairwell, if she isn't your roommate): Wait. Look again. That's not smoke. [v: ]
5. [fight: shade ×3, at @hostel] **[switch: @roomie]** Freeze the shades with the torch and push them
   out of the corridor. (Teaches torch-freeze then hit. Switch to @roomie to hold the light while you
   push; the AI does whichever you don't.)
   - BATURE: I'm holding the torch! I'm being brave! Mostly my hand is being brave! [v: ]
   - FIRDAUSI: Garba-the-lizard is gone. Garba never leaves. This is serious. [v: ]
   - KHALID (if roommate): Behind you. I've got this side. Don't argue. [v: ]
6. [quiet] The shades thin out into the courtyard like smoke into a drain. The light comes back.
   Someone on the floor laughs too loudly. Then everyone does.
   - CORRIDOR: Up NEPA! [v: ]
   - @ROOMIE: Tell me you saw them. Please tell me you saw them and it wasn't the beans. [v: ]
7. [talk: garba] Go and tell Malam Garba at the Senate. He's on night duty.
   - MALAM GARBA: Shades. Hm. [v: ]
   - MALAM GARBA: The old students called them that. I thought they had stopped coming. [v: ]
   - MALAM GARBA: Go to bed. Keep your torch. Tomorrow, ask the electrical people about fuses. [v: ]
   - BATURE: Malam, riddle me this: who knows a thing and says "hm" instead? [v: ]
   - MALAM GARBA: A porter who wants to sleep. Goodnight. [v: ]
7a. [quiet] Walk home past Mai Shayi's stall. The kettle is still on.
   - AUDU: You saw them. I can see it on your face. Tea is free tonight. Only tonight. [v: ]
   - BATURE: Free tea? Riddle me this: what's rarer than light in this school? [v: ]

**Choice.** None.

**If it goes wrong.** Knocked down by the shades: you wake on your bed with @roomie fanning you with a
textbook; the fight restarts at beat 5 with one fewer shade. No Sick Bay trip here.

**Rewards.** ₦500, 600 XP, torch upgrade (brighter beam), Community +1. Friendship @roomie +5.
Unlocks m5.
**Sets / pays.** Sets `saw_shades`, `garba_hm`. The fan stopping before the light pays in m23 ("the hum").
**Around it.**
