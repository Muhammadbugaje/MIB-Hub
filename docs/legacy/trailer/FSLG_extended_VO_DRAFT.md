# Freshers School Life: extended trailer voiceover (draft)

This is a starting point for you to rewrite in your own words. Watch `FSLG_extended_trailer.mp4` once with no
sound, then once with the music, and read these lines against the picture. The times are where each line
can start. Every cut is on the beat (100 bpm, one beat = 0.6 s), so a line that starts on a cut sits well.

**Recording:** one take from start to finish, 0:00 = the first frame of the video. Save it as
`docs/trailer/extended_voiceover.wav` (any sample rate, 16-bit or 24-bit WAV). The build mixes it in and
lowers the music about 12 dB under your voice while you speak.

**Voice:** warm and amused, like a big brother showing a fresher round. Smile on the funny lines; let the
laughs happen. In "The story" slow right down and drop your voice.

Lines in [brackets] are stage notes, not words to read.
