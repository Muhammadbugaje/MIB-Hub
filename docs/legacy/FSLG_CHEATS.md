# Freshers School Life (FSLG): cheat codes, the secret student and easter eggs

Keep this file private. These are the **default** codes. They can be changed, reset to these defaults and exported from the admin **Game Settings** page; if they have been changed there, the codes below no longer work.

The codes are not stored in the game's scripts. The page only receives scrambled fingerprints of them, so nobody can read them out of the browser.

## Entering a code

- **Keyboard:** just type the word while playing. No box opens and you do not need the phone. Capital letters and small letters both work.
- **Phone:** press **Q**, choose **Dial a code**, enter the dial code and press **Call**. The typed word also works in the dial box.

Using any code marks the save as "codes used". Whether such saves still appear on leaderboards and reports is a switch in Game Settings.

## The secret student: M.I. BUGAJE

Muhammad Iliyasu Bugaje is a hidden playable character in a white kaftan and cap. He is not shown on the "Who are you?" screen.

1. **Reveal him** on the "Who are you?" screen (start a **New student**):
   - Keyboard: press **↑ ↑ ↓ M I B** (click outside the name box first).
   - Phone: swipe **up, up, down, left, right, up**.
2. **Unlock him:** tap his picture and enter the secret code. On the site this is the code generated the first time you open Arcade Management (or the one you set there). A stand-in code works only on a local development copy (DEBUG on). The device remembers the unlock.

What he can do:

- Cannot be hurt or caught; security ignores him.
- Never tired or hungry, and never short of money.
- Starts at level 10, so every vehicle and the aircraft are open from the first minute.
- Runs faster, jumps about twice as high, and wins any fight with one hit.
- **]** opens the Maker's menu with every cheat as a button (on a phone: Phone → Dial a code).
- The **backslash key** skips the current mission step, so the whole 36-mission story can be test-run quickly. The **Pick any mission** cheat jumps straight to any mission.
- Other players in a multiplayer room see his name in gold.

## Easter eggs (8)

These stay hidden in the game itself. Spoilers follow.

1. **Konami code.** Press ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A for retro scan-line mode.
2. **EEHUB.** Type it during play to see the credits.
3. **The waving statue.** Wave (key 1) five times in a row at the Sculpture Garden. A statue waves back and leaves ₦2,000.
4. **Midnight dance.** Dance (key 2) at the Senate Building between 23:30 and 01:30.
5. **Sunrise at the dam.** Sit (key 3) by ABU Dam between 05:30 and 07:30 and wait about 15 seconds for a golden fish worth ₦5,000.
6. **The secret station.** Visit the Campus Radio Studio to unlock 8-Bit Buka on your vehicle radio.
7. **The hat on the roof.** Fly the aircraft over the Senate Building.
8. **A visiting physicist.** Between 12:00 and 14:00 an old scientist sits outside the Department of Physics. Talk to him.
