# Freshers School Life — filler phrase bank

Short, reusable campus phrases. When a character has a line nobody has recorded yet, the game shows the real
words in the speech bubble and plays one of these instead, picked to match the mood and the moment.
Anyone can record this bank. The **For everyone** sections are for every reader; then a man also reads
**Men only** and a woman also reads **Women only**.

Format, one phrase per line: `- phrase · mood · use`. Moods: happy, annoyed, sad, excited, neutral, worried,
flirty, angry-light, tired. Edit freely; ids come from the words, so re-run `sh tools/voice/run.sh` after a change.
