# Freshers School Life — Humour bank

Private to the owner. Lines for the team to record and for writers to drop into scenes. Every line is
original to this game, short, and kind: the joke is the situation, never a person's tribe, faith,
body or bank balance. Lines marked ★ are exclamations to be recorded as short sounds by several voices.

## 4. Bature's riddles (24)

Bature asks; most are silly; several land like truth. Tanko's riddles are the deep twin of these.

1. What has keys but can't open your hostel door? Your HOD's piano. (She has a piano. Ask her.)
2. What goes up when the rain comes down? Your umbrella. And your rent.
3. What has a neck but no head? A bottle of zobo. Also my last boyfriend's argument. (said to Firdausi)
4. What gets wetter the more it dries? A towel. And a fresher at a registration queue.
5. What can you catch but not throw? A cold. And feelings in the Love Garden.
6. What runs but never walks? Water from the tap. When it runs. Which is never.
7. What has hands but can't clap? A clock. And Corporal Peter at a match.
8. What comes once in a minute, twice in a moment, never in a thousand years? The letter M. Like Maker.
9. What gets bigger the more you take away? A hole. And your garri debt.
10. What belongs to you but others use it more? Your name. Ask Lucky.
11. What has one eye but can't see? A needle. Ask Alhaji Needle. It's ready Thursday.
12. What is full of holes but still holds water? A sponge. And our hostel roof, spiritually.
13. What can travel round the world while staying in a corner? A stamp. Mrs. Adebayo wants four.
14. What has to be broken before you can use it? An egg. And a fresher's pride.
15. What goes through towns and hills but never moves? The road to Phase 2. It just lies there.
16. What has words but never speaks? A book. Fatima's. Don't touch it.
17. What is always in front of you but can't be seen? The future. And your fringe.
18. What has many teeth but can't bite? A comb. Bolaji's. He bites anyway.
19. What can fill a room but takes no space? Light. (pause) …Light. That one wasn't a joke.
20. What disappears as soon as you say its name? Silence. (He says nothing. The joke lands.)
21. The more you have of it, the less you see. Darkness. Riddle me this: so why are we scared?
22. What is yours and still goes when you go? Your shadow. And your roommate, if you have garri.
23. What gets sharper the more you use it? Your brain. Mine is at the back of the class, resting.
24. What do you give that you keep after giving? Your word. (Year 5, at the dinner. Nobody laughs.)
