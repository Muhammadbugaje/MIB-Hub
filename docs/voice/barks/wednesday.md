# Barks: Wednesday
**Id:** npc.wednesday
**Voice:** A student about your age whom you only ever meet on Wednesdays (the engine only spawns them then). Calm, kind, a little uncanny: talks as if they have heard this conversation before, and gently mixes up "last week" and "next week". Never explains. Signs off "See you Wednesday."
