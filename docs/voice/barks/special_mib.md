# Barks: M.I. BUGAJE
**Id:** special.mib
**Voice:** The owner. A calm man in white and gold; very kind, very quiet, faintly suspicious. Speaks like a poet in short lines that can be read two or three ways. Never hurries, never explains himself, and is gone before the conversation feels finished. Rare: free-roam sightings at most once a month (bible §8.5).
