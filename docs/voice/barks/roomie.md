# Barks: {{ROOMIE}} (Bature or Firdausi)
**Id:** npc.roomie
**Voice:** Recorded by both roommates (Bature for a male player, Firdausi for a female player). Late teens to early twenties; the clown of the room, fast and warm, hides every feeling in a joke.
