# Meet Tanko

> Ask him anything. He'll answer: "Which?"

The riddler who sits by the garden gate, older than he acts and slyer than he looks. He asks riddles and almost never gives the answers.

### The voice

The riddler by the garden gate, older than he acts, playful and sly. Asks riddles and rarely gives answers; his favourite reply to everything is "Which?"

### What is this game?

It's a game you play in a web browser, about surviving five years at a Nigerian university. Please don't expect GTA. Nobody steals a car. The most dangerous vehicle in it is a three-wheeled keke taxi with a married couple arguing in the back, and the nearest thing to a boss fight is a queue.

You play a brand-new student, the Fresher. You register (four offices, four queues, four passport photographs), go broke by week three, live on garri, make friends for life, sit exams by torchlight because the power has gone again, and one day you graduate. Under the jokes there is a mystery: why the lights keep going off, and why a quiet old man has spent forty years in a garden keeping one of them on. Nobody gets hurt. The cure for the monster is company.

The university and the town are made up. The voices are friends and family, recording on their phones. Thank you for being one of them.

# How to record

**What you've got.** **66 lines**, about **588 words**. That's 14 mission lines and 52 common lines. Read straight through, it's **about 7 minutes** of talking. Nobody should do it in one sitting. A few sections a day is perfect.

**Change any word so it sounds like you.** Honestly. If a line doesn't sound right in your mouth, say it your way. If you think of a funnier one, record that as well. You are the expert on how Tanko talks.

#### Getting set up

- **A phone is fine.** Use its voice recorder app on the best quality setting, in aeroplane mode so nothing pings.
- **Pick a quiet, soft room.** A wardrobe full of clothes is the best studio in any house. Switch off fans and the TV.
- **Hold the phone about a hand-span from your mouth,** slightly to one side, and keep it there.

#### Reading the page

- **The big black words are your line.** Say only those.
- **The small orange words above a line are how to say it,** and sometimes who you are talking to. Don't read them out.
- **Small grey words are what somebody else says just before you,** so you know the moment. Don't read them out.
- **Words in [square brackets] are the English meaning.** Don't say them. If the Hausa, Pidgin or any other language is wrong, fix it. You know better than I do.
- **(name)** is where the game drops in the player's name: leave a short pause. **Bature/Firdausi** is the player's roommate: say the line once with each name.
- **Three dots (...) mean a beat of silence.**

#### Recording

- **One recording per coloured bar.** Start recording, read that bar's lines in order, stop. Then the next bar.
- **Leave about two seconds of silence between lines.** That's where I cut. No need to say the line numbers.
- **If you fluff a line,** pause and say it again. Don't stop the recording.
- **Got the energy?** Say a line twice, two different ways, and I'll pick the best one.
- **Shouting lines last,** and drink water.

#### Sending it

- Name each file after its coloured bar, like this: *Tanko - Mission 1*.
- Put everything in one folder and send me a Google Drive or WeTransfer link, with a photo of the signed last page. There is no deadline.

# Mission lines

In story order. Each coloured bar is one mission: its name, when it happens and what is going on. One recording per bar.

:: 1 | your normal voice | Riddle. What has six legs and cannot stand?
:: 2 | dry, deadpan | ((The roommate: “Two goats. Tired.”)) Which?

::: Mission 2 · Six Plaques | Year 1, second semester. A sculptor has noticed something odd about the Sculpture Garden: six plaques, and room for a seventh.

:: 3 | a whisper | Seven, some days.
:: 4 | dry, deadpan | ((Shamsu: “Four years of 'Which?' You never said!”)) Nobody asked me a question.
:: 5 | amused | They asked me riddles.
:: 6 | amused | ((The Fresher: “Who was the seventh cup for?”)) Ah. Now that… is a riddle. Which?

# Common lines

These are the short lines Tanko says around campus, outside the story: when the player walks up, bumps into you, hands you a present, or just passes by. The game picks one each time, so they never play in a row. Say each as if someone has just walked past.

::: Common 1 · Saying hello | The player has just walked up.
:: 1 | amused | Riddle. What has a gate but no wall? …Me. Hello.
:: 2 | dry, deadpan | You came. Which you? The one from yesterday?
:: 3 | amused | Sannu! [Hello!] Answer first, greeting after.

::: Common 2 · Saying goodbye
:: 1 | dry, deadpan | Go. But which way?
:: 2 | amused | Riddle for the road: what leaves and stays? …Your shadow.

::: Common 3 · Thank-yous and sorries
:: 1 | dry, deadpan | Thanks? Which for?

::: Common 4 · Walking with the player | Keeping up, waiting, and when the player goes the wrong way.
:: 1 | waiting for the player, dry, deadpan | Slow? Which slow? Snail slow, or Bursary slow?
:: 2 | the player went the wrong way, amused | Lost? What has many doors and one way out? This campus.

::: Common 5 · Quick answers | The player asks something and gets one of these back.
:: 1 | yes, dry, deadpan | Yes. Which yes?
:: 2 | no, amused | No. But only this one.
:: 3 | confused, dry, deadpan | Which?
:: 4 | confused, amused | Which 'he'? Which 'there'? Which 'nobody'?

::: Common 6 · Jokes and questions
:: 1 | a question | Riddle. What has ten mouths and was never fed?
:: 2 | a question, amused | Riddle. What gets wetter the more it dries? Not your towel. Which?
:: 3 | a joke, amused | What has hands, a face, and nowhere to go? Look up.
:: 4 | a joke, dry, deadpan | What do you lose by saying its name? Silence. Now we've lost it.
:: 5 | a joke, amused | What is full of holes and still holds water? The sponge. Too easy.
:: 6 | a joke, dry, deadpan | What is always in front of you and never seen? Your future. Which?

::: Common 7 · When something goes wrong | Bumps, falls, power cuts, rain and heat. NEPA is what everybody calls the power company, and so every power cut.
:: 1 | the lights go out, a whisper, during a power cut | What grows bigger the more you take away? The dark.
:: 2 | the lights come back, amused | What returns without travelling? Light. Ha! Which?
:: 3 | rain, amused, when it's raining | What falls and never breaks? Rain. What breaks and never falls? Day.
:: 4 | bumped into, amused | What bumps and does not say sorry? You. Answer me.

::: Common 8 · Food and money
:: 1 | dry, deadpan | What do you eat before you eat it? A promise from Mama Nkechi.

::: Common 9 · Getting a present | The player hands over a present. Some go down better than others.
:: 1 | likes the present, amused | A gift. What do you give and still keep? Your word. Thank you.
:: 2 | not keen on the present, dry, deadpan | What is given and not wanted? …No riddle. Just this.
:: 3 | loves the present, warm | Tea. Hot. Hm. For this, you may ask me an easy one.

::: Common 10 · Talking to yourself | Said to nobody in particular as the player walks past.
:: 1 | your normal voice | What runs all night and never leaves the hostel? The tap.
:: 2 | amused | What queues longest and moves least? The Bursary. Ha.
:: 3 | dry, deadpan | What is lit by one and seen by all? …Hm. Not yet.
:: 4 | your normal voice | What has a neck and no head? The bottle. Also some lecturers.
:: 5 | a whisper | What can you hold without touching? A promise. Hm.
:: 6 | amused | What goes up and never comes down? Your age. And mall prices.

::: Common 11 · Late at night | After dark. Quieter.
:: 1 | a whisper, only at night | What has one eye and watches all night? The moon. Or the porter.

::: Common 12 · Year 1: arriving | Everything is new. Queues, friends, the first power cuts.
:: 1 | to yourself | What has six legs and cannot stand? Still no answer? Good.
:: 2 | to yourself, amused | What do freshers carry that weighs nothing? Fear. Put it down.

::: Common 13 · Year 2: the mall | A shiny new mall has opened, and the lights keep going off.
:: 1 | to yourself, dry, deadpan | What shines brightest when everything else is dark? Which building?
:: 2 | to yourself, amused | What do you plug in and pull out? Your friend's shop. Ha.

::: Common 14 · Year 3: the strike | The lecturers are on strike. No lectures, an empty campus, no money.
:: 1 | to yourself | What is empty and still full? A hall in a strike.
:: 2 | to yourself, dry, deadpan | What waits in a forest for forty years? …Which forest?

::: Common 15 · Year 4: fame | Viral videos, an election, and work placements.
:: 1 | to yourself, amused | What wears your face and is not you? Ask the cameras.
:: 2 | to yourself, dry, deadpan | Nobody asked me a question. Only riddles. Still true.
:: 3 | looking back, warm, after “The Old Eye (Heist 2)” | Six cups up that stair. Riddle: who drank the seventh? Which?

::: Common 16 · Year 5: leaving | Final year. Projects, job offers, and everyone counting the days.
:: 1 | to yourself | What has ten mouths and was never fed? You know now.
:: 2 | to yourself, sad, low | What leaves every year and comes back new? Finalists. Hm.

::: Common 17 · After graduation | The story is over. The player has come back to visit.
:: 1 | hello, amused | Riddle: what was stopped and now runs? Listen. Which?
:: 2 | to yourself, warm | What does a riddler do when nobody is lost? Rest. Hm.

::: Common 18 · Special days: term time | Days on the campus calendar. Allowance day is when money from home arrives.
:: 1 | freshers' week, amused | New faces! Riddle: who is new every year? Which?
:: 2 | exam week, dry, deadpan, in exam week | What has questions and no riddles? Exams. Boring.
:: 3 | results day | What is on the board and not in your head? Your grade. Hm.

::: Common 19 · Special days: the big ones | Save any shouting for last.
:: 1 | cup final day, amused | What has one ball and twenty-two owners? Which team?
:: 2 | election week, dry, deadpan | What promises everything and owns nothing? A poster.

::: Common 20 · Special days: seasons and holidays | Harmattan is the dry, dusty season.
:: 1 | harmattan, amused | What dries everything and is never thirsty? Harmattan.
