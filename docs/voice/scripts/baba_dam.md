# Meet Baba Dam

> Fish don't hurry. Neither does he.

The old fisherman of the dam outside campus. He has a boat, a biscuit tin and all the time in the world, and he says more with a pause than most lecturers say in a semester.

### The voice

Old fisherman of Kwari Dam, slow and salty. Speaks like the water moves: a few words, long pauses. "Fish don't hurry."

### What is this game?

It's a game you play in a web browser, about surviving five years at a Nigerian university. Please don't expect GTA. Nobody steals a car. The most dangerous vehicle in it is a three-wheeled keke taxi with a married couple arguing in the back, and the nearest thing to a boss fight is a queue.

You play a brand-new student, the Fresher. You register (four offices, four queues, four passport photographs), go broke by week three, live on garri, make friends for life, sit exams by torchlight because the power has gone again, and one day you graduate. Under the jokes there is a mystery: why the lights keep going off, and why a quiet old man has spent forty years in a garden keeping one of them on. Nobody gets hurt. The cure for the monster is company.

The university and the town are made up. The voices are friends and family, recording on their phones. Thank you for being one of them.

# How to record

**What you've got.** **62 lines**, about **490 words**. That's 15 mission lines and 47 common lines. Read straight through, it's **about 6 minutes** of talking. Nobody should do it in one sitting. A few sections a day is perfect.

**Change any word so it sounds like you.** Honestly. If a line doesn't sound right in your mouth, say it your way. If you think of a funnier one, record that as well. You are the expert on how Baba Dam talks.

#### Getting set up

- **A phone is fine.** Use its voice recorder app on the best quality setting, in aeroplane mode so nothing pings.
- **Pick a quiet, soft room.** A wardrobe full of clothes is the best studio in any house. Switch off fans and the TV.
- **Hold the phone about a hand-span from your mouth,** slightly to one side, and keep it there.

#### Reading the page

- **The big black words are your line.** Say only those.
- **The small orange words above a line are how to say it,** and sometimes who you are talking to. Don't read them out.
- **Small grey words are what somebody else says just before you,** so you know the moment. Don't read them out.
- **Words in [square brackets] are the English meaning.** Don't say them. If the Hausa, Pidgin or any other language is wrong, fix it. You know better than I do.
- **(name)** is where the game drops in the player's name: leave a short pause. **Bature/Firdausi** is the player's roommate: say the line once with each name.
- **Three dots (...) mean a beat of silence.**

#### Recording

- **One recording per coloured bar.** Start recording, read that bar's lines in order, stop. Then the next bar.
- **Leave about two seconds of silence between lines.** That's where I cut. No need to say the line numbers.
- **If you fluff a line,** pause and say it again. Don't stop the recording.
- **Got the energy?** Say a line twice, two different ways, and I'll pick the best one.
- **Shouting lines last,** and drink water.

#### Sending it

- Name each file after its coloured bar, like this: *Baba Dam - Mission 1*.
- Put everything in one folder and send me a Google Drive or WeTransfer link, with a photo of the signed last page. There is no deadline.

# Mission lines

In story order. Each coloured bar is one mission: its name, when it happens and what is going on. One recording per bar.

::: Mission 1 · Baba Dam's Pier | Year 1, second semester. A Sunday at the dam with an old fisherman who does not hurry.
:: 1 | dry, deadpan, to the roommate | ((The roommate: “Riddle: what is free, wet, and full of fi—”)) Fish don't hurry. Pier don't either.
:: 2 | to the roommate | Four planks. You broke one. So: four planks.
:: 3 | to the Fresher | ((The Fresher: “What's that? On the cliff.”)) A tower. One day.
:: 4 | dry, deadpan, to the Fresher | They brought the stakes when my hair was black.
:: 5 | dry, deadpan, to the Fresher | Then they ran out of wood.

::: Mission 2 · The Dam at Midnight | Year 1, second semester. Midnight at the dam. The water is singing.
:: 1 | a whisper, to the roommate | Listen.
:: 2 | to the roommate | ((The roommate: “That's the garden's sound. Under WATER.”)) Forty years it sang soft. This month, loud.

::: Mission 3 · Touch Grass | Year 4, second semester. A day on the dam with no phone. Baba Dam keeps it in a biscuit tin.
:: 1 | dry, deadpan | Phone.
:: 2 | warm | Fish don't hurry. Today, you also.
:: 3 | your normal voice | His sister.
:: 4 | firm | Ask him nothing.
:: 5 | dry, deadpan, to Dr. Zainab | ((Dr. Zainab: “At the garden bench. …What is she looking at?”)) Cats don't hurry either. Eat.

::: Mission 4 · The Eel's Bargain | Year 4, second semester. Five in the morning in the Life Sciences lab, with Firdausi and an electric fish.
:: 1 | a whisper | ((The Maker: “"The water of life is hidden in the land of darkness."”)) Look at the fish. Like somebody used to feed them.

::: Mission 5 · Two by Sky and Water | Year 5, second semester. Relighting two more: one at the observatory, one out on the dam.
:: 1 | warm | She remembers the kilishi.
:: 2 | dry, deadpan | Fish don't hurry. But they don't forget.

# Common lines

These are the short lines Baba Dam says around campus, outside the story: when the player walks up, bumps into you, hands you a present, or just passes by. The game picks one each time, so they never play in a row. Say each as if someone has just walked past.

::: Common 1 · Saying hello | The player has just walked up.
:: 1 | dry, deadpan | Fish don't hurry. Sit.
:: 2 | your normal voice | Sannu. [Hello.] Mind the fourth plank.
:: 3 | dry, deadpan | You. Shoes off. The pier is old.

::: Common 2 · Saying goodbye
:: 1 | your normal voice | Go slowly. The road is not a river, but still.
:: 2 | dry, deadpan | Go. The fish are shy of talkers.

::: Common 3 · Thank-yous and sorries
:: 1 | dry, deadpan | Thank the fish.

::: Common 4 · Teasing | Friendly. The joke is never unkind.
:: 1 | amused | You cast like you're throwing a phone. Again.

::: Common 5 · Walking with the player | Keeping up, waiting, and when the player goes the wrong way.
:: 1 | dry, deadpan | Slow? Good. Slower.

::: Common 6 · Quick answers | The player asks something and gets one of these back.
:: 1 | no, dry, deadpan | No. Not today. The water said.
:: 2 | yes | Yes. Bring bait.

::: Common 7 · Jokes and questions
:: 1 | dry, deadpan | Swimming lesson. Lesson one: float. Lesson two: float.
:: 2 | amused | You caught a fish. Or the fish caught you. We'll see.

::: Common 8 · Winning and losing | After a game, a match or a bet.
:: 1 | after winning, proud | Big one. Don't shout. The others will hear.
:: 2 | after losing, dry, deadpan | Gone. Fish don't hurry, but they don't stay either.

::: Common 9 · When something goes wrong | Bumps, falls, power cuts, rain and heat. NEPA is what everybody calls the power company, and so every power cut.
:: 1 | rain, when it's raining | Rain on the water. Fish come up to look.
:: 2 | a hot day, dry, deadpan, on a hot day | Hot? Feet in the water. Fish won't mind. Much.
:: 3 | after a fall, dry, deadpan | In the water? Float. I told you. Float.

::: Common 10 · Food and money
:: 1 | warm | Catch it, clean it, fire it. Best meal on campus. No queue.

::: Common 11 · Getting a present | The player hands over a present. Some go down better than others.
:: 1 | likes the present | Hooks. Good ones. Hm.
:: 2 | not keen on the present, dry, deadpan | A phone case that floats. My phone does not float. I do not have one.
:: 3 | loves the present, warm | Kilishi. Ah. Some here will be glad of it.

::: Common 12 · Talking to yourself | Said to nobody in particular as the player walks past.
:: 1 | your normal voice | Water is low. The fish are thinking.
:: 2 | dry, deadpan | Forty years on this pier. It has lost more planks than me.
:: 3 | dry, deadpan | That tower on the cliff. One day. They ran out of wood.
:: 4 | a whisper | Listen. …No. Just water today.
:: 5 | amused | The kingfisher is better than me. I tell nobody.

::: Common 13 · Late at night | After dark. Quieter.
:: 1 | firm, only at night | No swimming at night. The water belongs to others then.

::: Common 14 · Year 1: arriving | Everything is new. Queues, friends, the first power cuts.
:: 1 | to yourself | Four planks. You fixed them. The pier remembers.
:: 2 | to yourself, a whisper | The water hums this month. Loud. Don't swim at the pier.
:: 3 | looking back, dry, deadpan, after “The Dam at Midnight” | You gave dried meat to an eel. She liked you. Bad sign.

::: Common 15 · Year 2: the mall | A shiny new mall has opened, and the lights keep going off.
:: 1 | to yourself, dry, deadpan | The mall has a fountain. Hm. This one is bigger.
:: 2 | to yourself | She is bigger this year. The eel. Eats more kilishi than me.

::: Common 16 · Year 3: the strike | The lecturers are on strike. No lectures, an empty campus, no money.
:: 1 | to yourself | Strike. Good. More fish for the ones who stayed.
:: 2 | to yourself, dry, deadpan | Someone is building again on the cliff. One rung. Hm.

::: Common 17 · Year 4: fame | Viral videos, an election, and work placements.
:: 1 | to yourself, firm | The spillway stinks. She is angry. I would be too.
:: 2 | to yourself | Phone in the tin. Fish don't hurry. Today you also.
:: 3 | looking back, warm, after “The Eel's Bargain” | She lets you swim past now. That girl talked to her right.
:: 4 | looking back, firm, after “Touch Grass” | That photograph on my boat. Ask him nothing.

::: Common 18 · Year 5: leaving | Final year. Projects, job offers, and everyone counting the days.
:: 1 | to yourself | Last year. Come and fish. The exam will still be there.
:: 2 | to yourself, a whisper | Fish don't hurry. But they don't forget.

::: Common 19 · After graduation | The story is over. The player has come back to visit.
:: 1 | to yourself, warm | He comes at sunset now. Sits. We say nothing. Very good.
:: 2 | to yourself, amused | The tower has another rung. At this pace, my grandson's son.

::: Common 20 · Special days: term time | Days on the campus calendar. Allowance day is when money from home arrives.
:: 1 | exam week, dry, deadpan, in exam week | Exams? Read here. Fish don't talk. Unlike your roommate.
:: 2 | market day | Market day. Smoked fish. Early, before Hajiya prices them.

::: Common 21 · Special days: the big ones | Save any shouting for last.
:: 1 | the strike, dry, deadpan, during the strike | No lectures? Come. The dam never closed.

::: Common 22 · Special days: seasons and holidays | Harmattan is the dry, dusty season.
:: 1 | Ramadan, warm, in Ramadan | Ramadan Kareem. Fresh fish at sunset. I keep you one.
:: 2 | harmattan | Harmattan. The water smokes in the morning. Fish come deep.
