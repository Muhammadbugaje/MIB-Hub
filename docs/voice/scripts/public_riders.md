# Meet The Riders

> Philosophers of the pothole.

Okada riders, keke drivers, bus drivers and their conductors: shouting routes, squeezing seats, and offering life advice at speed.

### The voice

Okada riders, keke drivers, danfo and shuttle drivers and their conductors; shouting routes, squeezing seats, philosophers of the pothole.

### What is this game?

It's a game you play in a web browser, about surviving five years at a Nigerian university. Please don't expect GTA. Nobody steals a car. The most dangerous vehicle in it is a three-wheeled keke taxi with a married couple arguing in the back, and the nearest thing to a boss fight is a queue.

You play a brand-new student, the Fresher. You register (four offices, four queues, four passport photographs), go broke by week three, live on garri, make friends for life, sit exams by torchlight because the power has gone again, and one day you graduate. Under the jokes there is a mystery: why the lights keep going off, and why a quiet old man has spent forty years in a garden keeping one of them on. Nobody gets hurt. The cure for the monster is company.

The university and the town are made up. The voices are friends and family, recording on their phones. Thank you for being one of them.

# How to record

**What you've got.** **45 lines**, about **435 words**. Read straight through, it's **about 5 minutes** of talking. Nobody should do it in one sitting. A few sections a day is perfect.

**Change any word so it sounds like you.** Honestly. If a line doesn't sound right in your mouth, say it your way. If you think of a funnier one, record that as well. **More voices are welcome:** it's a crowd, so share the lines out among friends and family.

#### Getting set up

- **A phone is fine.** Use its voice recorder app on the best quality setting, in aeroplane mode so nothing pings.
- **Pick a quiet, soft room.** A wardrobe full of clothes is the best studio in any house. Switch off fans and the TV.
- **Hold the phone about a hand-span from your mouth,** slightly to one side, and keep it there.

#### Reading the page

- **The big black words are your line.** Say only those.
- **The small orange words above a line are how to say it,** and sometimes who you are talking to. Don't read them out.
- **Small grey words are what somebody else says just before you,** so you know the moment. Don't read them out.
- **Words in [square brackets] are the English meaning.** Don't say them. If the Hausa, Pidgin or any other language is wrong, fix it. You know better than I do.
- **(name)** is where the game drops in the player's name: leave a short pause. **Bature/Firdausi** is the player's roommate: say the line once with each name.
- **Three dots (...) mean a beat of silence.**

#### Recording

- **One recording per coloured bar.** Start recording, read that bar's lines in order, stop. Then the next bar.
- **Leave about two seconds of silence between lines.** That's where I cut. No need to say the line numbers.
- **If you fluff a line,** pause and say it again. Don't stop the recording.
- **Got the energy?** Say a line twice, two different ways, and I'll pick the best one.
- **Shouting lines last,** and drink water.

#### Sending it

- Name each file after its coloured bar, like this: *The Riders - Common 1*.
- Put everything in one folder and send me a Google Drive or WeTransfer link, with a photo of the signed last page. There is no deadline.

# Common lines

These are the short lines The Riders says around campus, outside the story: when the player walks up, bumps into you, hands you a present, or just passes by. The game picks one each time, so they never play in a row. Say each as if someone has just walked past.

::: Common 1 · Saying hello | The player has just walked up.
:: 1 | your normal voice | Where you dey go? [Where are you going?]
:: 2 | excited | Keke! Keke! Gate, Senate, New Site!
:: 3 | excited | Shuttle to New Site! One more! One more! Two more!

::: Common 2 · Thank-yous and sorries
:: 1 | warm | Na gode! [Thank you!] Safe journey!

::: Common 3 · When something goes wrong | Bumps, falls, power cuts, rain and heat. NEPA is what everybody calls the power company, and so every power cut.
:: 1 | bumped into, sharp, quick | Ah! Watch your leg! The keke is not a footstool!
:: 2 | rain, dry, deadpan, when it's raining | Rain! Fare has doubled! Don't look at me, look at the sky!

::: Common 4 · Talking to yourself | Said to nobody in particular as the player walks past.
:: 1 | excited | Senate one-fifty! Mall two hundred! Far places, negotiate!
:: 2 | amused | Enter, enter. There is space. Space is a mindset.
:: 3 | dry, deadpan | Shift small. Small. Small again. Perfect. Now we are four.
:: 4 | dry, deadpan | Oga, your change is coming. It's on the next trip.
:: 5 | amused | Everybody bring exact. Change is on strike.
:: 6 | firm | Hold well. This road has opinions.
:: 7 | amused | Pothole there! Pothole here! The pothole is the road.
:: 8 | your normal voice | Last shuttle to New Site! After this one, it's your legs.
:: 9 | dry, deadpan | Fuel went up again. So did I. Two-fifty now.
:: 10 | sad, low | Where is my conductor? He went to buy bread one hour ago.

::: Common 5 · Late at night | After dark. Quieter.
:: 1 | dry, deadpan, only at night | Night price is different. Darkness is a premium service.
:: 2 | a whisper, only at night | Ghost okada at three in the morning? Abeg [please], I'm going home.
:: 3 | only at night | I don't pass by the garden at night. Not fear. Just… sleep.

::: Common 6 · Year 1: arriving | Everything is new. Queues, friends, the first power cuts.
:: 1 | to yourself, proud, during a power cut | Traffic lights are off again. Me, I'm the traffic light now.
:: 2 | to yourself, amused | One fresher asked me for a receipt. Receipt! For keke!
:: 3 | to yourself, amused | I carried a goat yesterday. Better passenger than you. Didn't argue.

::: Common 7 · Year 2: the mall | A shiny new mall has opened, and the lights keep going off.
:: 1 | to yourself, excited | Mall! Mall! Everybody's going to look at the escalator!
:: 2 | to yourself, bored, flat | Fuel queue from here to tomorrow. Fare is now 'how much you get?'
:: 3 | to yourself, amused | Okada school? Who taught that one? He's riding like an apology.

::: Common 8 · Year 3: the strike | The lecturers are on strike. No lectures, an empty campus, no money.
:: 1 | to yourself, sad, low | The strike has finished my customers. I carry bread and memories.
:: 2 | to yourself, sad, low | Empty campus. I'm riding slowly so it looks busy.
:: 3 | to yourself | Motor park is full. Everybody's going home till they call it off.

::: Common 9 · Year 4: fame | Viral videos, an election, and work placements.
:: 1 | to yourself, amused | Every passenger is filming. Am I trending? Tell me if I'm trending.
:: 2 | to yourself, dry, deadpan | Campaign sticker in front, another one at the back. I'm neutral.
:: 3 | to yourself, sad, low | Candidates giving free rides. My income has resigned.

::: Common 10 · Year 5: leaving | Final year. Projects, job offers, and everyone counting the days.
:: 1 | to yourself, excited | Finalists everywhere! Hold your gown, the wind is fast!
:: 2 | to yourself, warm | Five years I carry you, now you're going? Pay double. For memory.

::: Common 11 · Special days: term time | Days on the campus calendar. Allowance day is when money from home arrives.
:: 1 | freshers' week, excited | Freshers! Senate this way! Hostel that way! Future: pay me.
:: 2 | exam week, proud | Exam hall? Hold tight. I'll fly you. Safely.
:: 3 | results day | Results day. Half my passengers are singing, half are silent.
:: 4 | allowance day, amused | Allowance day. First the bank, then the mall, then back to me.
:: 5 | market day, excited | Gidan Kwari! Thursday market! Shift small!

::: Common 12 · Special days: the big ones | Save any shouting for last.
:: 1 | the strike, excited | Kaduna! Kano! Jos! Home is calling, I'm the phone!
:: 2 | cup final day, excited | Stadium! Stadium! Match at four! Wear your colours!
:: 3 | election week, sad, low | A candidate put a speaker on my keke. My ears are on strike.
:: 4 | graduation day, excited | Convocation! Families, big bags! Canopy! Canopy this way!

::: Common 13 · Special days: seasons and holidays | Harmattan is the dry, dusty season.
:: 1 | Ramadan, excited | Last trips before iftar! Hurry, hurry, sunset doesn't wait!
:: 2 | Christmas, amused | Christmas travellers! Bags on top, people inside, chickens… we'll see.
:: 3 | harmattan, dry, deadpan | Harmattan dust. I can't see the road. The road can't see me. Fair.
