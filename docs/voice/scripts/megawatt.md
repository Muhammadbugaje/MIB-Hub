# Meet Chief Megawatt

> Let us not be emotional. Let us be commercial.

The businessman who built the shiny new mall. Big smile, warm voice, always selling, never quite illegal. He is not a villain. He honestly believes everything he says, which is worse.

### The voice

A big-hearted businessman in his fifties, warm baritone, always smiling, always selling. Generous, sharp, never quite illegal. He believes what he says. "Let us not be emotional. Let us be commercial."

### What is this game?

It's a game you play in a web browser, about surviving five years at a Nigerian university. Please don't expect GTA. Nobody steals a car. The most dangerous vehicle in it is a three-wheeled keke taxi with a married couple arguing in the back, and the nearest thing to a boss fight is a queue.

You play a brand-new student, the Fresher. You register (four offices, four queues, four passport photographs), go broke by week three, live on garri, make friends for life, sit exams by torchlight because the power has gone again, and one day you graduate. Under the jokes there is a mystery: why the lights keep going off, and why a quiet old man has spent forty years in a garden keeping one of them on. Nobody gets hurt. The cure for the monster is company.

The university and the town are made up. The voices are friends and family, recording on their phones. Thank you for being one of them.

# How to record

**What you've got.** **64 lines**, about **595 words**. That's 19 mission lines and 45 common lines. Read straight through, it's **about 7 minutes** of talking. Nobody should do it in one sitting. A few sections a day is perfect.

**Change any word so it sounds like you.** Honestly. If a line doesn't sound right in your mouth, say it your way. If you think of a funnier one, record that as well. You are the expert on how Chief Megawatt talks.

#### Getting set up

- **A phone is fine.** Use its voice recorder app on the best quality setting, in aeroplane mode so nothing pings.
- **Pick a quiet, soft room.** A wardrobe full of clothes is the best studio in any house. Switch off fans and the TV.
- **Hold the phone about a hand-span from your mouth,** slightly to one side, and keep it there.

#### Reading the page

- **The big black words are your line.** Say only those.
- **The small orange words above a line are how to say it,** and sometimes who you are talking to. Don't read them out.
- **Small grey words are what somebody else says just before you,** so you know the moment. Don't read them out.
- **Words in [square brackets] are the English meaning.** Don't say them. If the Hausa, Pidgin or any other language is wrong, fix it. You know better than I do.
- **(name)** is where the game drops in the player's name: leave a short pause. **Bature/Firdausi** is the player's roommate: say the line once with each name.
- **Three dots (...) mean a beat of silence.**

#### Recording

- **One recording per coloured bar.** Start recording, read that bar's lines in order, stop. Then the next bar.
- **Leave about two seconds of silence between lines.** That's where I cut. No need to say the line numbers.
- **If you fluff a line,** pause and say it again. Don't stop the recording.
- **Got the energy?** Say a line twice, two different ways, and I'll pick the best one.
- **Shouting lines last,** and drink water.

#### Sending it

- Name each file after its coloured bar, like this: *Chief Megawatt - Mission 1*.
- Put everything in one folder and send me a Google Drive or WeTransfer link, with a photo of the signed last page. There is no deadline.

# Common lines

These are the short lines Chief Megawatt says around campus, outside the story: when the player walks up, bumps into you, hands you a present, or just passes by. The game picks one each time, so they never play in a row. Say each as if someone has just walked past.

::: Common 1 · Saying hello | The player has just walked up.
:: 1 | warm | My friend! Welcome to the light!
:: 2 | proud | Ah, the student who costs me money. Good afternoon!
:: 3 | amused | Come, come. Stand in my air-conditioning. It's free. Today.
:: 4 | warm | Sannu da zuwa! [Welcome!] Have you seen the new fountain?

::: Common 2 · Saying goodbye
:: 1 | warm | Go well. Think of me in the dark. Kindly.
:: 2 | amused | Bye-bye! Tell your friends. Tell your enemies. Everybody shops.

::: Common 3 · Thank-yous and sorries
:: 1 | warm | Thank you! Gratitude is the first investment.

::: Common 4 · Being nice | Compliments. Mean them.
:: 1 | warm | You have a business head. Wasted on honesty, but a business head.

::: Common 5 · Teasing | Friendly. The joke is never unkind.
:: 1 | amused | Your friend Mubarak. Talented boy. Annoying, but talented.
:: 2 | amused | You are frowning at my lights. Lights are not a crime, my friend.

::: Common 6 · Quick answers | The player asks something and gets one of these back.
:: 1 | yes, warm | Yes! Done! Let us shake. Firmly. Commercially.
:: 2 | no, amused | No. But I say it with love and a discount.
:: 3 | maybe | Maybe. Send it in writing. With formatting.
:: 4 | refusing, warm | Let us not be emotional. Let us be commercial.

::: Common 7 · Jokes and questions
:: 1 | a question, warm | Tell me. What do students want? Apart from light and food.
:: 2 | a joke, amused | My generator has a name. My wife says I love it too much.

::: Common 8 · When something goes wrong | Bumps, falls, power cuts, rain and heat. NEPA is what everybody calls the power company, and so every power cut.
:: 1 | the lights go out, proud | Light gone outside? Not in here. Never blinks!
:: 2 | the lights go out | Somebody always pays for light. I simply print the receipt.
:: 3 | the lights come back, dry, deadpan | NEPA is back. My competitor. We are cordial.
:: 4 | a hot day, warm | Hot? Come in. The cold is on the house. The drinks are not.

::: Common 9 · Food and money
:: 1 | your normal voice | Money is not evil. It is just loud. I like a quiet profit.

::: Common 10 · Getting a present | The player hands over a present. Some go down better than others.
:: 1 | likes the present, warm | A gift! I will remember this. I remember everything.
:: 2 | not keen on the present, amused | A candle. Very funny. Very, very funny.
:: 3 | loves the present, shy | A drawing of my first shop. Where did you… I'm being emotional.

::: Common 11 · Talking to yourself | Said to nobody in particular as the player walks past.
:: 1 | proud | Kwari Mall. Where the light never blinks!
:: 2 | amused | Who did this formatting? It's beautiful. Hire them.
:: 3 | warm | I started with one kiosk and a stubborn generator. Look now.

::: Common 12 · Year 2: the mall | A shiny new mall has opened, and the lights keep going off.
:: 1 | to yourself, proud | Grand opening! Free jollof until the jollof says stop!
:: 2 | to yourself | My locks went missing from the plant room. Curious. Very curious.
:: 3 | looking back, warm, after “Unplug Iron Mama (Heist 1)” | You cost me a generator. I named her myself. No hard feelings.
:: 4 | teasing, amused | Your shop is in a basement. Mine has escalators. Just saying.

::: Common 13 · Year 3: the strike | The lecturers are on strike. No lectures, an empty campus, no money.
:: 1 | the strike | Strike! The mall is full. Bored students buy shawarma.
:: 2 | the strike, warm | Tell the lecturers: their families shop here free this week.

::: Common 14 · Year 4: fame | Viral videos, an election, and work placements.
:: 1 | to yourself | An influencer asked for free things. I gave him free advice.
:: 2 | to yourself, amused | Your Plug Store has two branches? …He will go far. Annoying.

::: Common 15 · Year 5: leaving | Final year. Projects, job offers, and everyone counting the days.
:: 1 | to yourself, warm | Your coil. I would fund all of it. My meter on it, that's all.
:: 2 | to yourself, shy | I offered. You said no. I respect it. I don't like it. I respect it.
:: 3 | looking back, after “Megawatt's Offer” | Where do I plug it? …I'm asking as a friend.

::: Common 16 · Special days: term time | Days on the campus calendar. Allowance day is when money from home arrives.
:: 1 | allowance day, excited | Allowance day! My favourite holiday!
:: 2 | market day | Market day. Hajiya Ladi's prices. Good. Competition is healthy.

::: Common 17 · Special days: the big ones | Save any shouting for last.
:: 1 | graduation day, warm | Graduates! Ten per cent off suits. Twenty if you cry.

::: Common 18 · Special days: seasons and holidays | Harmattan is the dry, dusty season.
:: 1 | Ramadan, warm | Iftar tables at the mall entrance. Free. Not a promotion. Mostly.
:: 2 | Christmas, proud | Christmas lights! Ten thousand bulbs! Count them!
:: 3 | Valentine's Day, amused | Valentine's! Two-for-one! Love is commercial too.
:: 4 | harmattan, amused | Harmattan. Dust on my glass doors. I will sue the wind.
