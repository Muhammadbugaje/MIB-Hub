# Meet Wednesday

> See you Wednesday. You will, too.

A student about the player's age who is only ever met on a Wednesday. Calm, kind and slightly uncanny, as if this conversation has happened before. Never explains.

### The voice

Calm, kind, a little uncanny. Talks as if they have heard this conversation before, and gently mixes up "last week" and "next week". Signs off "See you Wednesday."

### What is this game?

It's a game you play in a web browser, about surviving five years at a Nigerian university. Please don't expect GTA. Nobody steals a car. The most dangerous vehicle in it is a three-wheeled keke taxi with a married couple arguing in the back, and the nearest thing to a boss fight is a queue.

You play a brand-new student, the Fresher. You register (four offices, four queues, four passport photographs), go broke by week three, live on garri, make friends for life, sit exams by torchlight because the power has gone again, and one day you graduate. Under the jokes there is a mystery: why the lights keep going off, and why a quiet old man has spent forty years in a garden keeping one of them on. Nobody gets hurt. The cure for the monster is company.

The university and the town are made up. The voices are friends and family, recording on their phones. Thank you for being one of them.

# How to record

**What you've got.** **64 lines**, about **583 words**. That's 13 mission lines and 51 common lines. Read straight through, it's **about 7 minutes** of talking. Nobody should do it in one sitting. A few sections a day is perfect.

**Change any word so it sounds like you.** Honestly. If a line doesn't sound right in your mouth, say it your way. If you think of a funnier one, record that as well. You are the expert on how Wednesday talks.

#### Getting set up

- **A phone is fine.** Use its voice recorder app on the best quality setting, in aeroplane mode so nothing pings.
- **Pick a quiet, soft room.** A wardrobe full of clothes is the best studio in any house. Switch off fans and the TV.
- **Hold the phone about a hand-span from your mouth,** slightly to one side, and keep it there.

#### Reading the page

- **The big black words are your line.** Say only those.
- **The small orange words above a line are how to say it,** and sometimes who you are talking to. Don't read them out.
- **Small grey words are what somebody else says just before you,** so you know the moment. Don't read them out.
- **Words in [square brackets] are the English meaning.** Don't say them. If the Hausa, Pidgin or any other language is wrong, fix it. You know better than I do.
- **(name)** is where the game drops in the player's name: leave a short pause. **Bature/Firdausi** is the player's roommate: say the line once with each name.
- **Three dots (...) mean a beat of silence.**

#### Recording

- **One recording per coloured bar.** Start recording, read that bar's lines in order, stop. Then the next bar.
- **Leave about two seconds of silence between lines.** That's where I cut. No need to say the line numbers.
- **If you fluff a line,** pause and say it again. Don't stop the recording.
- **Got the energy?** Say a line twice, two different ways, and I'll pick the best one.
- **Shouting lines last,** and drink water.

#### Sending it

- Name each file after its coloured bar, like this: *Wednesday - Mission 1*.
- Put everything in one folder and send me a Google Drive or WeTransfer link, with a photo of the signed last page. There is no deadline.

# Mission lines

In story order. Each coloured bar is one mission: its name, when it happens and what is going on. One recording per bar.

::: Mission 1 · First Lecture, Wrong Theatre | Year 1, first semester. The first lecture, in the wrong theatre. The two theatres look the same and their signs are swapped.
:: 1 | to Dr. Okonkwo | See you Wednesday.

::: Mission 2 · Radio Silence | Year 1, second semester. Bature auditions for campus radio. The station is two rooms with egg boxes on the walls.
:: 1 | your normal voice | See you Wednesday.
:: 2 | warm | ((The Fresher: “It IS Wednesday.”)) I know.

::: Mission 3 · Those Who Stayed | Year 3, during the strike. Two weeks into the strike. Most people have gone home. These are the ones who stayed.
:: 1 | your normal voice | ((The Students: “They'll call it off by Friday. My uncle said.”)) It does that when the place is empty. Things loop.
:: 2 | warm | ((The Fresher: “You saw it too?”)) See you Wednesday.

::: Mission 4 · Echo | Year 4, first semester. A copy of the Fresher is on the basketball court at night, doing the dance slightly wrong.
:: 1 | to the Fresher | There are Echoes of everybody now.
:: 2 | warm, to the Fresher | Have you noticed whose they never make?
:: 3 | amused, to the Fresher | ((The Fresher: “Whose?”)) See you Wednesday.

::: Mission 5 · Hock Day | Year 4, first semester. The same Wednesday, three times. It is also the roommate's birthday.
:: 1 | warm, to the Fresher | You noticed. Good.
:: 2 | to the Fresher | I've done this one eleven… eleven times.
:: 3 | amused, to the Fresher | ((The Fresher: “How do we stop it?”)) I never have. Ask me something easier.
:: 4 | dry, deadpan, to the Fresher | Beans are good at 12:40. Terrible by one.
:: 5 | a whisper, to the Fresher | It isn't angry, you know. It's just holding on.

# Common lines

These are the short lines Wednesday says around campus, outside the story: when the player walks up, bumps into you, hands you a present, or just passes by. The game picks one each time, so they never play in a row. Say each as if someone has just walked past.

::: Common 1 · Saying hello | The player has just walked up.
:: 1 | warm | Oh, hello. You're early. Or I'm late. One of us.
:: 2 | your normal voice | Hi. It's Wednesday. Of course it is.
:: 3 | warm | There you are. I was just about to say hello. I've said it now.
:: 4 | amused | Hello again. Or for the first time. Which one is it for you?

::: Common 2 · Saying goodbye
:: 1 | warm | See you Wednesday.
:: 2 | amused | See you Wednesday. Don't be late. You won't be.

::: Common 3 · Thank-yous and sorries
:: 1 | warm | Thank you. You said that last time too. I mean I did.

::: Common 4 · Being nice | Compliments. Mean them.
:: 1 | warm | You're kinder than you were. Or than you will be. It suits you.

::: Common 5 · Teasing | Friendly. The joke is never unkind.
:: 1 | amused | You're going to drop that. …There. Told you.
:: 2 | dry, deadpan | You always pick the long queue. Every Wednesday. It's charming.

::: Common 6 · Walking with the player | Keeping up, waiting, and when the player goes the wrong way.
:: 1 | waiting for the player | It's all right. I'm good at waiting. Six days at a time.
:: 2 | the player went the wrong way | Not that way. You went that way last time. I mean next time.

::: Common 7 · Quick answers | The player asks something and gets one of these back.
:: 1 | your normal voice | What day is it for you? Just checking.
:: 2 | dry, deadpan | Who? There was nobody there fa. Just us. Just Wednesday.

::: Common 8 · Jokes and questions
:: 1 | a question | Have you had the beans? Twelve-forty is the time. Not one.
:: 2 | a joke, amused | What's the best day of the week? I can't say. I'm biased.

::: Common 9 · When something goes wrong | Bumps, falls, power cuts, rain and heat. NEPA is what everybody calls the power company, and so every power cut.
:: 1 | a hot day, dry, deadpan | Hot. It was hot last Wednesday too. It'll be hot next. Consistent.
:: 2 | rain | Rain at four. Short. Then the smell. You'll like the smell.

::: Common 10 · Food and money
:: 1 | dry, deadpan | Beans at the Spot. Good at twelve-forty. Terrible by one.

::: Common 11 · Getting a present | The player hands over a present. Some go down better than others.
:: 1 | likes the present, warm | For me? Thank you. I'll keep it till next week. It'll keep.
:: 2 | loves the present, a whisper | A calendar. With every day on it. …That's very kind.

::: Common 12 · Talking to yourself | Said to nobody in particular as the player walks past.
:: 1 | your normal voice | Tuesday was long. I hear.
:: 2 | a whisper | Hm. That clock again. Still six minutes past.

::: Common 13 · Late at night | After dark. Quieter.
:: 1 | a whisper | Wednesday nights are quiet. Thursday's waiting. It's polite.

::: Common 14 · Year 1: arriving | Everything is new. Queues, friends, the first power cuts.
:: 1 | hello | New here? Me too. I think. See you Wednesday.
:: 2 | to yourself, amused | Theatre Gabas or Yamma? Gabas. Today it's Gabas. Trust me.
:: 3 | looking back, warm, after “First Lecture, Wrong Theatre” | You found the right theatre. Eventually. Wednesday helps.
:: 4 | confused | You've been here a week? Feels like longer. For me.

::: Common 15 · Year 2: the mall | A shiny new mall has opened, and the lights keep going off.
:: 1 | to yourself, warm | The radio's louder this year. Good. The nights need it.
:: 2 | looking back, warm, after “Radio Silence” | You listened to the whole show. I know. I was listening too.
:: 3 | teasing, amused | You're busy now. You missed two Wednesdays. I didn't.

::: Common 16 · Year 3: the strike | The lecturers are on strike. No lectures, an empty campus, no money.
:: 1 | to yourself | Empty halls. Things loop when places are empty. Mind your step.
:: 2 | looking back, after “Those Who Stayed” | Those who stayed. I stayed too. In my way.
:: 3 | the strike, warm | Strike. Every day feels like Wednesday. I don't mind.
:: 4 | being nice, warm | You keep showing up. That's most of it.

::: Common 17 · Year 4: fame | Viral videos, an election, and work placements.
:: 1 | to yourself | So many Echoes this year. They never get the eyes right. Look at the eyes.
:: 2 | looking back, warm, after “Hock Day” | You noticed. That loop. Good. Not many do.
:: 3 | looking back, amused, after “Hock Day” | Eleven times, then twelve. You were quicker than me. Much quicker.
:: 4 | teasing, dry, deadpan, after “Hock Day” | You threaded the timetable. Show-off. Nicely done.
:: 5 | a question | What's today for you? Good. Hold on to it.

::: Common 18 · Year 5: leaving | Final year. Projects, job offers, and everyone counting the days.
:: 1 | to yourself, warm | Last Wednesdays. I'll count them. I'm good at counting.
:: 2 | being nice, warm | You're nearly at the end. It's a good end. Mostly.
:: 3 | goodbye, warm | After convocation… well. See you Wednesday. Somewhere.
:: 4 | looking back, after “Hock Day” | You stopped asking me what day it is. That's growing up.

::: Common 19 · Special days: term time | Days on the campus calendar. Allowance day is when money from home arrives.
:: 1 | freshers' week, warm | Freshers' Week. Every year it's new. Every year it's the same new.
:: 2 | exam week | Exam on Wednesday? Bring two pencils. One breaks. It always does.
:: 3 | results day, amused | Results. You'll pass. I'm not guessing. I'm just patient.

::: Common 20 · Special days: the big ones | Save any shouting for last.
:: 1 | cup final day | The final's on Saturday? Shame. Tell me how it goes. On Wednesday.
:: 2 | graduation day, warm | A Wednesday convocation. Somebody planned that for me. Thank you.

::: Common 21 · Special days: seasons and holidays | Harmattan is the dry, dusty season.
:: 1 | Ramadan, warm | Iftar on a Wednesday. The dates are always sweeter midweek. Don't ask why.
:: 2 | harmattan, a whisper | Harmattan. The days blur. Easy to lose a Wednesday in the haze.
