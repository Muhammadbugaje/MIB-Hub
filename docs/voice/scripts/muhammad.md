# Meet Muhammad

> Says sorry before he helps you. And after.

A soft-spoken young man in a plain white shirt who turns up exactly when he is needed and is gone when you look back. He knows things he shouldn't. He never explains. Players will argue about him for years, and you never settle it for them.

### The voice

Young man, soft-spoken and unhurried, a little shy; says "sorry" before he helps and after. Never raises his voice. Every line is quieter than you expect.

### What is this game?

It's a game you play in a web browser, about surviving five years at a Nigerian university. Please don't expect GTA. Nobody steals a car. The most dangerous vehicle in it is a three-wheeled keke taxi with a married couple arguing in the back, and the nearest thing to a boss fight is a queue.

You play a brand-new student, the Fresher. You register (four offices, four queues, four passport photographs), go broke by week three, live on garri, make friends for life, sit exams by torchlight because the power has gone again, and one day you graduate. Under the jokes there is a mystery: why the lights keep going off, and why a quiet old man has spent forty years in a garden keeping one of them on. Nobody gets hurt. The cure for the monster is company.

The university and the town are made up. The voices are friends and family, recording on their phones. Thank you for being one of them.

# How to record

**What you've got.** **52 lines**, about **339 words**. That's 30 mission lines and 22 common lines. Read straight through, it's **about 5 minutes** of talking. Nobody should do it in one sitting. A few sections a day is perfect.

**Change any word so it sounds like you.** Honestly. If a line doesn't sound right in your mouth, say it your way. If you think of a funnier one, record that as well. You are the expert on how Muhammad talks.

#### Getting set up

- **A phone is fine.** Use its voice recorder app on the best quality setting, in aeroplane mode so nothing pings.
- **Pick a quiet, soft room.** A wardrobe full of clothes is the best studio in any house. Switch off fans and the TV.
- **Hold the phone about a hand-span from your mouth,** slightly to one side, and keep it there.

#### Reading the page

- **The big black words are your line.** Say only those.
- **The small orange words above a line are how to say it,** and sometimes who you are talking to. Don't read them out.
- **Small grey words are what somebody else says just before you,** so you know the moment. Don't read them out.
- **Words in [square brackets] are the English meaning.** Don't say them. If the Hausa, Pidgin or any other language is wrong, fix it. You know better than I do.
- **(name)** is where the game drops in the player's name: leave a short pause. **Bature/Firdausi** is the player's roommate: say the line once with each name.
- **Three dots (...) mean a beat of silence.**

#### Recording

- **One recording per coloured bar.** Start recording, read that bar's lines in order, stop. Then the next bar.
- **Leave about two seconds of silence between lines.** That's where I cut. No need to say the line numbers.
- **If you fluff a line,** pause and say it again. Don't stop the recording.
- **Got the energy?** Say a line twice, two different ways, and I'll pick the best one.
- **Shouting lines last,** and drink water.

#### Sending it

- Name each file after its coloured bar, like this: *Muhammad - Mission 1*.
- Put everything in one folder and send me a Google Drive or WeTransfer link, with a photo of the signed last page. There is no deadline.

# Mission lines

In story order. Each coloured bar is one mission: its name, when it happens and what is going on. One recording per bar.

::: Mission 1 · Queue Theory | Year 1, the first week. Registration: four offices, four queues, four passport photographs.
:: 1 | warm | ((Mr. Clement: “Network.”)) Excuse me. Sorry.
:: 2 | warm | Don't take the shortcut, (name).
:: 3 | your normal voice | The gate is locked on Tuesdays.

::: Mission 2 · Test Week, One Lamp | Year 1, first semester, test week. Three tests in four days, and a tutorial for forty people under one working lamp.
:: 1 | warm | ((The Fresher: “No. No no no.”)) Excuse me. Sorry.
:: 2 | your normal voice | Question four is the long one. Start there.
:: 3 | your normal voice | Leave one till last. It only looks easy.
:: 4 | shy | Sorry. I shouldn't. You looked worried.

::: Mission 3 · The Dam at Midnight | Year 1, second semester. Midnight at the dam. The water is singing.
:: 1 | warm | You'll be all right. I checked. Sorry.

::: Mission 4 · Meter Reader | Year 2, first semester. Following a white Works van at night to find out who is switching the lights off.
:: 1 | warm, to the Fresher | Excuse me. Sorry.
:: 2 | to the Fresher | The van turns left. It always turns left.
:: 3 | sad, low, to the Fresher | He doesn't like to. He waits a moment first.
:: 4 | warm, to the Fresher | ((The Fresher: “How do you—”)) Sorry. You'd better go.

:: 3 | warm | Someone will get to it.

::: Mission 7 · Past the Last Streetlight | Year 3, during the strike. Walking past the last streetlight on the ring road, into the forest.

::: Mission 8 · IT Letter | Year 4, first semester. Fourth year starts in the Registry queue. This time the Fresher brought a folding chair.

::: Mission 9 · Seoul or Stay | Year 4, second semester. Dr. Zainab has an offer from Seoul and an email she hasn't sent.

# Common lines

These are the short lines Muhammad says around campus, outside the story: when the player walks up, bumps into you, hands you a present, or just passes by. The game picks one each time, so they never play in a row. Say each as if someone has just walked past.

::: Common 1 · Year 1: arriving | Everything is new. Queues, friends, the first power cuts.
:: 1 | hello, warm | Excuse me. Sorry. Your lace is undone.
:: 2 | sorry, shy | Sorry. I just knew.
:: 3 | the player went the wrong way, warm | Not that stair. Sorry. The other one.
:: 4 | rain, warm, when it's raining | It stops at the gate. Sorry. Walk slowly.

::: Common 2 · Year 2: the mall | A shiny new mall has opened, and the lights keep going off.
:: 1 | thank you, shy | No, no. Thank you. Sorry. Go on.
:: 2 | late at night, a whisper, only at night | Keep to the lamps tonight. Sorry.
:: 3 | goodbye, warm | This is my turning. Go well. Sorry.

::: Common 3 · Year 3: the strike | The lecturers are on strike. No lectures, an empty campus, no money.
:: 1 | to yourself, warm | Quiet, isn't it. That's all right.
:: 2 | hello, warm | You're carrying too much. Sorry. Give me one.
:: 3 | confused, shy | Sorry. I can't say.

::: Common 4 · Year 4: fame | Viral videos, an election, and work placements.
:: 1 | sorry, shy | Sorry. I shouldn't know that. I just do.
:: 2 | bumped into, warm | Sorry. Sorry. My fault. Up you come.
:: 3 | to yourself, warm | You'll be all right. I checked twice. Sorry.

::: Common 5 · Year 5: leaving | Final year. Projects, job offers, and everyone counting the days.
:: 1 | to yourself, warm | Nearly there. Sorry. I can't carry this one.
:: 2 | late at night, warm, only at night | Your light's still on. Sleep. Sorry.
:: 3 | goodbye, warm | Go on. They're waiting for you.

::: Common 6 · After graduation | The story is over. The player has come back to visit.
:: 1 | hello, shy | Excuse me. Sorry. Is this the way to the Senate?
:: 2 | a question, shy | Sorry. Where do freshers queue for the Bursary?
:: 3 | a question, shy | Sorry. Have we met? …No. Sorry.
:: 4 | a question, warm | Excuse me. Which floor is six? The porter said six.
:: 5 | thank you, warm | Thank you. Sorry. Everybody here is so kind.
:: 6 | goodbye, shy | Sorry. I'm meant to be somewhere. Thank you.
