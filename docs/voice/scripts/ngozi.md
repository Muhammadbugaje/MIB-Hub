# Meet Coach Ngozi

> Garri is not a food group.

The gym trainer. She counts everything out loud and believes every body is good at something. Tough, upbeat and never unkind.

### The voice

Gym trainer, Igbo woman in her thirties, upbeat, counts everything out loud, believes every body is good at something. Tough, never unkind. "Garri is not a food group."

### What is this game?

It's a game you play in a web browser, about surviving five years at a Nigerian university. Please don't expect GTA. Nobody steals a car. The most dangerous vehicle in it is a three-wheeled keke taxi with a married couple arguing in the back, and the nearest thing to a boss fight is a queue.

You play a brand-new student, the Fresher. You register (four offices, four queues, four passport photographs), go broke by week three, live on garri, make friends for life, sit exams by torchlight because the power has gone again, and one day you graduate. Under the jokes there is a mystery: why the lights keep going off, and why a quiet old man has spent forty years in a garden keeping one of them on. Nobody gets hurt. The cure for the monster is company.

The university and the town are made up. The voices are friends and family, recording on their phones. Thank you for being one of them.

# How to record

**What you've got.** **43 lines**, about **380 words**. That's 3 mission lines and 40 common lines. Read straight through, it's **about 5 minutes** of talking. Nobody should do it in one sitting. A few sections a day is perfect.

**Change any word so it sounds like you.** Honestly. If a line doesn't sound right in your mouth, say it your way. If you think of a funnier one, record that as well. You are the expert on how Coach Ngozi talks.

#### Getting set up

- **A phone is fine.** Use its voice recorder app on the best quality setting, in aeroplane mode so nothing pings.
- **Pick a quiet, soft room.** A wardrobe full of clothes is the best studio in any house. Switch off fans and the TV.
- **Hold the phone about a hand-span from your mouth,** slightly to one side, and keep it there.

#### Reading the page

- **The big black words are your line.** Say only those.
- **The small orange words above a line are how to say it,** and sometimes who you are talking to. Don't read them out.
- **Small grey words are what somebody else says just before you,** so you know the moment. Don't read them out.
- **Words in [square brackets] are the English meaning.** Don't say them. If the Hausa, Pidgin or any other language is wrong, fix it. You know better than I do.
- **(name)** is where the game drops in the player's name: leave a short pause. **Bature/Firdausi** is the player's roommate: say the line once with each name.
- **Three dots (...) mean a beat of silence.**

#### Recording

- **One recording per coloured bar.** Start recording, read that bar's lines in order, stop. Then the next bar.
- **Leave about two seconds of silence between lines.** That's where I cut. No need to say the line numbers.
- **If you fluff a line,** pause and say it again. Don't stop the recording.
- **Got the energy?** Say a line twice, two different ways, and I'll pick the best one.
- **Shouting lines last,** and drink water.

#### Sending it

- Name each file after its coloured bar, like this: *Coach Ngozi - Mission 1*.
- Put everything in one folder and send me a Google Drive or WeTransfer link, with a photo of the signed last page. There is no deadline.

# Mission lines

In story order. Each coloured bar is one mission: its name, when it happens and what is going on. One recording per bar.

::: Mission 1 · Sly's Last Stand | Year 1, second semester. Sly challenges the Fresher to a fair spar, with rules and a referee.
:: 1 | warm, to Taty | The scale speaks. Don't take it personally.
:: 2 | firm, to Taty | Three lifts. On the beat. Then you eat properly.
:: 3 | dry, deadpan, to Taty | Garri is not a food group. It is a cry for help.

## Common lines

These are the short lines Coach Ngozi says around campus, outside the story: when the player walks up, bumps into you, hands you a present, or just passes by. The game picks one each time, so they never play in a row. Say each as if someone has just walked past.

::: Common 1 · Saying hello | The player has just walked up.
:: 1 | excited | You came! Ah! Warm-up first. Arms. Go.
:: 2 | warm | Nno! [Welcome!] Water bottle? Good. Towel? Good. Excuses? Leave them.
:: 3 | amused | Look who remembered the gym exists.

::: Common 2 · Saying goodbye
:: 1 | warm | Stretch tonight. Your legs will thank me tomorrow. Or curse me.
:: 2 | firm | Go and eat properly. Properly. Not garri and hope.

::: Common 3 · Thank-yous and sorries
:: 1 | warm | Thank me with three more reps.

::: Common 4 · Being nice | Compliments. Mean them.
:: 1 | proud | Clean form! Somebody has been practising!
:: 2 | warm | Every body is good at something. Yours just found it.

::: Common 5 · Teasing | Friendly. The joke is never unkind.
:: 1 | amused | You groaned before you lifted. Lift first, groan after.
:: 2 | firm | Garri is not a food group. It is a cry for help.
:: 3 | amused | You took a selfie between sets. The set is jealous.

::: Common 6 · Walking with the player | Keeping up, waiting, and when the player goes the wrong way.
:: 1 | walking along, excited | With me! One, two, three, breathe!
:: 2 | waiting for the player, firm | On the beat! The beat is not waiting!

::: Common 7 · Quick answers | The player asks something and gets one of these back.
:: 1 | yes, excited | Yes! One more!
:: 2 | no, firm | No. Not that weight. Not yet. We build.

::: Common 8 · Jokes and questions
:: 1 | your normal voice | What did you eat today? Count it. Out loud.

::: Common 9 · Winning and losing | After a game, a match or a bet.
:: 1 | after winning, excited | Personal best! Write it down! I'm writing it down!
:: 2 | after losing, warm | Didn't lift it today. Tomorrow you will. That's the whole secret.

::: Common 10 · When something goes wrong | Bumps, falls, power cuts, rain and heat. NEPA is what everybody calls the power company, and so every power cut.
:: 1 | bumped into | Sit. Shake it off. The scale speaks, the floor doesn't.
:: 2 | a hot day, firm | Hot? Water. Shade. Then we do it slowly.
:: 3 | the lights go out, amused | No light? Push-ups don't need light. Down!

::: Common 11 · Food and money
:: 1 | warm | Beans! Protein! Ah, you're learning!

::: Common 12 · Getting a present | The player hands over a present. Some go down better than others.
:: 1 | likes the present, warm | Groundnuts. Good protein. Don't eat them all at once. I will.
:: 2 | not keen on the present, dry, deadpan | Soft drink. In my gym. You want me to faint?
:: 3 | loves the present, excited | A new skipping rope! Oh, you will regret this tomorrow. In a good way.

::: Common 13 · Talking to yourself | Said to nobody in particular as the player walks past.
:: 1 | your normal voice | Three lifts. On the beat. Then you eat properly.
:: 2 | amused | Who left the dumbbell on the bench? It's not a pillow.

::: Common 14 · Year 1: arriving | Everything is new. Queues, friends, the first power cuts.
:: 1 | looking back, warm, after “Sly's Last Stand” | The scale spoke that day. Don't take it personally. Look at you now.
:: 2 | to yourself, warm | Freshers come in January. Leave in February. Be a March person.

::: Common 15 · Year 2: the mall | A shiny new mall has opened, and the lights keep going off.
:: 1 | to yourself, amused | Mall gym has mirrors everywhere. Mine has one. It's honest.

::: Common 16 · Year 3: the strike | The lecturers are on strike. No lectures, an empty campus, no money.
:: 1 | the strike, firm | Strike? Perfect. No lectures. Squats.
:: 2 | the strike, warm | Free classes this month. Bring your roommate. Bring your neighbour.

::: Common 17 · Year 4: fame | Viral videos, an election, and work placements.
:: 1 | to yourself, dry, deadpan | No filming in the gym. Your muscles are not content.

::: Common 18 · Year 5: leaving | Final year. Projects, job offers, and everyone counting the days.
:: 1 | to yourself, warm | Final year. Stress? Lift it. Literally.

::: Common 19 · Special days: term time | Days on the campus calendar. Allowance day is when money from home arrives.
:: 1 | exam week, warm | Exam week? Ten minutes walking clears the head. Doctor's orders. Mine.

::: Common 20 · Special days: the big ones | Save any shouting for last.
:: 1 | cup final day, proud | Cup season. Half the team trained here. You can tell.

::: Common 21 · Special days: seasons and holidays | Harmattan is the dry, dusty season.
:: 1 | Ramadan | Fasting? Light stretches only. After iftar, we talk.
:: 2 | Christmas, amused | Christmas rice. January gym. I've seen this film.
:: 3 | Valentine's Day, amused | Valentine's? Love yourself. Ten squats. That's self-love.
:: 4 | harmattan, excited | Cold morning jog! Bonus points! My points, but still!
