# Meet Tunde

> Doesn't talk much. Hands you a drawing instead.

A quiet Fine Arts student who draws where other people would speak. Most of his lines are two to five words. When he does say something, it lands.

### The voice

Early twenties, Fine Arts, quiet artist. Draws instead of talking: most lines are two to five words, shy, often with the scratch of a pencil under them. When he does speak, it lands.

### What is this game?

It's a game you play in a web browser, about surviving five years at a Nigerian university. Please don't expect GTA. Nobody steals a car. The most dangerous vehicle in it is a three-wheeled keke taxi with a married couple arguing in the back, and the nearest thing to a boss fight is a queue.

You play a brand-new student, the Fresher. You register (four offices, four queues, four passport photographs), go broke by week three, live on garri, make friends for life, sit exams by torchlight because the power has gone again, and one day you graduate. Under the jokes there is a mystery: why the lights keep going off, and why a quiet old man has spent forty years in a garden keeping one of them on. Nobody gets hurt. The cure for the monster is company.

The university and the town are made up. The voices are friends and family, recording on their phones. Thank you for being one of them.

# How to record

**What you've got.** **57 lines**, about **365 words**. That's 5 mission lines and 52 common lines. Read straight through, it's **about 5 minutes** of talking. Nobody should do it in one sitting. A few sections a day is perfect.

**Change any word so it sounds like you.** Honestly. If a line doesn't sound right in your mouth, say it your way. If you think of a funnier one, record that as well. You are the expert on how Tunde talks.

#### Getting set up

- **A phone is fine.** Use its voice recorder app on the best quality setting, in aeroplane mode so nothing pings.
- **Pick a quiet, soft room.** A wardrobe full of clothes is the best studio in any house. Switch off fans and the TV.
- **Hold the phone about a hand-span from your mouth,** slightly to one side, and keep it there.

#### Reading the page

- **The big black words are your line.** Say only those.
- **The small orange words above a line are how to say it,** and sometimes who you are talking to. Don't read them out.
- **Small grey words are what somebody else says just before you,** so you know the moment. Don't read them out.
- **Words in [square brackets] are the English meaning.** Don't say them. If the Hausa, Pidgin or any other language is wrong, fix it. You know better than I do.
- **(name)** is where the game drops in the player's name: leave a short pause. **Bature/Firdausi** is the player's roommate: say the line once with each name.
- **Three dots (...) mean a beat of silence.**

#### Recording

- **One recording per coloured bar.** Start recording, read that bar's lines in order, stop. Then the next bar.
- **Leave about two seconds of silence between lines.** That's where I cut. No need to say the line numbers.
- **If you fluff a line,** pause and say it again. Don't stop the recording.
- **Got the energy?** Say a line twice, two different ways, and I'll pick the best one.
- **Shouting lines last,** and drink water.

#### Sending it

- Name each file after its coloured bar, like this: *Tunde - Mission 1*.
- Put everything in one folder and send me a Google Drive or WeTransfer link, with a photo of the signed last page. There is no deadline.

# Mission lines

In story order. Each coloured bar is one mission: its name, when it happens and what is going on. One recording per bar.

::: Mission 1 · Test Week, One Lamp | Year 1, first semester, test week. Three tests in four days, and a tutorial for forty people under one working lamp.
:: 1 | shy, to the Fresher | ((The Fresher: “Who's that? Behind me.”)) I draw what's there.
:: 2 | a whisper, to the Fresher | …I don't remember that being there.

::: Mission 2 · Final Crit | Year 4, second semester. Aleeya's degree show opens at six. It is two o'clock, and things are going wrong.
:: 1 | shy, to the Dean of Environmental Design | I tried eleven times to paint what was in it.
:: 2 | a whisper, to the Dean of Environmental Design | It kept… not being there.

::: Mission 3 · The Mirror | Year 4, second semester. The mall after closing. A hundred reflections in the glass, all slightly late.
:: 1 | shy, to the Maker | ((The Maker: “Your counterfeit was hopelessly, magnificently two-dimensional.”)) It couldn't draw.

## Common lines

These are the short lines Tunde says around campus, outside the story: when the player walks up, bumps into you, hands you a present, or just passes by. The game picks one each time, so they never play in a row. Say each as if someone has just walked past.

::: Common 1 · Saying hello | The player has just walked up.
:: 1 | shy | …Hi. Hold still.
:: 2 | shy | Oh. Hello. Done. You moved.
:: 3 | warm | …Hey. Drew you yesterday. From memory.

::: Common 2 · Saying goodbye
:: 1 | shy | …Bye. Keep your chin like that.

::: Common 3 · Thank-yous and sorries
:: 1 | thank you, shy | …Thanks. Here. A drawing.
:: 2 | sorry, shy | Sorry. Pencil on your sleeve.

::: Common 4 · Being nice | Compliments. Mean them.
:: 1 | shy | Good face. For drawing.
:: 2 | warm | …Your hands. Nice shapes.

::: Common 5 · Teasing | Friendly. The joke is never unkind.
:: 1 | amused | …You blink a lot. Very hard to draw.

::: Common 6 · Walking with the player | Keeping up, waiting, and when the player goes the wrong way.
:: 1 | waiting for the player, shy | …Take your time. I'm sketching.
:: 2 | walking along, shy | …I'll walk. I'll draw the road.
:: 3 | the player went the wrong way | …Not there. Art block. Behind the gallery.

::: Common 7 · Quick answers | The player asks something and gets one of these back.
:: 1 | confused, shy | …Hm? I was shading.
:: 2 | confused, dry, deadpan | …Who? There was nobody there fa. Nobody to draw.
:: 3 | yes, shy | …Mm. Yes.
:: 4 | no, shy | …No. Sorry.

::: Common 8 · Jokes and questions
:: 1 | shy | …Can I draw you? Two minutes. Five.

::: Common 9 · Winning and losing | After a game, a match or a bet.
:: 1 | warm | …We won. Drew it already.

::: Common 10 · When something goes wrong | Bumps, falls, power cuts, rain and heat. NEPA is what everybody calls the power company, and so every power cut.
:: 1 | bumped into, shy | …Ah. My pencil. It's fine.
:: 2 | the lights go out, a whisper | …Dark. I'll draw the dark.
:: 3 | rain, warm | …Rain. Ink runs. It's nice.

::: Common 11 · Food and money
:: 1 | shy | …Puff-puff. I drew it first. Then ate it.

::: Common 12 · Getting a present | The player hands over a present. Some go down better than others.
:: 1 | likes the present, shy | …A sketchbook. Thank you. Thank you.
:: 2 | not keen on the present, shy | …Oh. Thank you. It's… shiny.
:: 3 | loves the present, excited | …Charcoal. The good one. …Wow.

::: Common 13 · Talking to yourself | Said to nobody in particular as the player walks past.
:: 1 | your normal voice | …Scratch. Scratch. …There.
:: 2 | shy | I draw what's there.

::: Common 14 · Late at night | After dark. Quieter.
:: 1 | a whisper | …Night lines are softer.

::: Common 15 · Year 1: arriving | Everything is new. Queues, friends, the first power cuts.
:: 1 | to yourself, shy | …Drew the lamp queue. Forty moths. And you.
:: 2 | looking back, shy, after “Test Week, One Lamp” | …Drew Shamsu under the one lamp. Everybody looks like moths.
:: 3 | being nice, shy | …You looked tired in the exam. Good drawing.
:: 4 | teasing, amused | …You ran to the wrong theatre. I drew it. Twice.

::: Common 16 · Year 2: the mall | A shiny new mall has opened, and the lights keep going off.
:: 1 | to yourself, shy | …Drew the mall. Then crossed it out.
:: 2 | being nice, warm | …You helped the trader with her stall. Drew that.
:: 3 | looking back, shy, after “Sly Goes Straight” | …Scored the haircut. Ten. Abbas said nine and a half.

::: Common 17 · Year 3: the strike | The lecturers are on strike. No lectures, an empty campus, no money.
:: 1 | to yourself | …Drew everybody who stayed. Ran out of paper.
