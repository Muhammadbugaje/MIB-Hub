# Meet The Freshers

> Nervous, overdressed, and calling everybody "senior".

Brand-new students, seventeen to nineteen. Loud in a group, tiny on their own, and carrying a file everywhere they go.

### The voice

Seventeen to nineteen, nervous and overdressed, loud in groups and tiny alone; calls everyone "senior", carries a file everywhere.

### What is this game?

It's a game you play in a web browser, about surviving five years at a Nigerian university. Please don't expect GTA. Nobody steals a car. The most dangerous vehicle in it is a three-wheeled keke taxi with a married couple arguing in the back, and the nearest thing to a boss fight is a queue.

You play a brand-new student, the Fresher. You register (four offices, four queues, four passport photographs), go broke by week three, live on garri, make friends for life, sit exams by torchlight because the power has gone again, and one day you graduate. Under the jokes there is a mystery: why the lights keep going off, and why a quiet old man has spent forty years in a garden keeping one of them on. Nobody gets hurt. The cure for the monster is company.

The university and the town are made up. The voices are friends and family, recording on their phones. Thank you for being one of them.

# How to record

**What you've got.** **67 lines**, about **705 words**. That's 4 mission lines and 63 common lines. Read straight through, it's **about 10 minutes** of talking. Nobody should do it in one sitting. A few sections a day is perfect.

**Change any word so it sounds like you.** Honestly. If a line doesn't sound right in your mouth, say it your way. If you think of a funnier one, record that as well. **More voices are welcome:** it's a crowd, so share the lines out among friends and family.

#### Getting set up

- **A phone is fine.** Use its voice recorder app on the best quality setting, in aeroplane mode so nothing pings.
- **Pick a quiet, soft room.** A wardrobe full of clothes is the best studio in any house. Switch off fans and the TV.
- **Hold the phone about a hand-span from your mouth,** slightly to one side, and keep it there.

#### Reading the page

- **The big black words are your line.** Say only those.
- **The small orange words above a line are how to say it,** and sometimes who you are talking to. Don't read them out.
- **Small grey words are what somebody else says just before you,** so you know the moment. Don't read them out.
- **Words in [square brackets] are the English meaning.** Don't say them. If the Hausa, Pidgin or any other language is wrong, fix it. You know better than I do.
- **(name)** is where the game drops in the player's name: leave a short pause. **Bature/Firdausi** is the player's roommate: say the line once with each name.
- **Three dots (...) mean a beat of silence.**

#### Recording

- **One recording per coloured bar.** Start recording, read that bar's lines in order, stop. Then the next bar.
- **Leave about two seconds of silence between lines.** That's where I cut. No need to say the line numbers.
- **If you fluff a line,** pause and say it again. Don't stop the recording.
- **Got the energy?** Say a line twice, two different ways, and I'll pick the best one.
- **Shouting lines last,** and drink water.

#### Sending it

- Name each file after its coloured bar, like this: *The Freshers - Mission 1*.
- Put everything in one folder and send me a Google Drive or WeTransfer link, with a photo of the signed last page. There is no deadline.

# Mission lines

In story order. Each coloured bar is one mission: its name, when it happens and what is going on. One recording per bar.

::: Mission 1 · Welcome, Fresher | Year 1, the first week. The first day. The family car at the gate, one bag, a runaway goat, and a note under the door that night.
:: 1 | excited | Faaa!

::: Mission 2 · Belle First | Year 1, the first week. Cooking jollof on a borrowed hotplate. Then the lights go out for the first time.

## Common lines

These are the short lines The Freshers says around campus, outside the story: when the player walks up, bumps into you, hands you a present, or just passes by. The game picks one each time, so they never play in a row. Say each as if someone has just walked past.

::: Common 1 · Saying hello | The player has just walked up.
:: 1 | shy | Good morning, senior. Sorry, good afternoon. Is it afternoon?
:: 2 | shy | Senior, please, is this Theatre Gabas or Theatre Yamma?
:: 3 | excited | Senior! You remember me? From the queue? The long one?

::: Common 2 · Thank-yous and sorries
:: 1 | warm | Thank you, senior! When I'm a senior, I'll show somebody too.

::: Common 3 · Walking with the player | Keeping up, waiting, and when the player goes the wrong way.
:: 1 | shy | Senior, I followed you because you looked like you knew.
:: 2 | sad, low | I've passed this goat three times. It's judging me.
:: 3 | shy | Is Giginya that way, or is that way also that way?

::: Common 4 · Jokes and questions
:: 1 | shy | Please, where do they sell the photocopy of the photocopy?
:: 2 | shy | Senior, how many passport photos is 'enough'?

::: Common 5 · When something goes wrong | Bumps, falls, power cuts, rain and heat. NEPA is what everybody calls the power company, and so every power cut.
:: 1 | the lights go out, scared | Ahn ahn! NEPA! On my first week!
:: 2 | the lights go out, amused | My mum said 'make sure there's light'. Mummy, I tried.
:: 3 | rain, sad, low, when it's raining | Nobody told me the rain here has a personality.

::: Common 6 · Talking to yourself | Said to nobody in particular as the player walks past.
:: 1 | your normal voice | My mum packed six tins of milk. I have nowhere to put my clothes.
:: 2 | amused | …and he said 'fresher, where is your file?' I said, what file?
:: 3 | scared | Is it true they lock the library with you inside?
:: 4 | bored, flat | Third queue today. I'm starting to know these people's families.
:: 5 | your normal voice | Clearance, registry, bursary, then clearance again. It's a circle.
:: 6 | amused | I wrote my matric number on my hand. Then I washed my hand.
:: 7 | excited | They said free meat pie at orientation. I came for knowledge. And pie.
:: 8 | sad, low | My phone is on two percent and my map is on zero.
:: 9 | your normal voice | Which one is the Clock Tower? The one that's not moving? Ah, okay.

::: Common 7 · Late at night | After dark. Quieter.
:: 1 | a whisper, only at night | Why is everybody's torch pointing at the floor? Is it a rule?
:: 2 | scared, only at night | My roommate said don't take the garden path at night. He didn't say why.
:: 3 | a whisper, only at night | Is the noticeboard always this full at midnight?

::: Common 8 · Year 1: arriving | Everything is new. Queues, friends, the first power cuts.
:: 1 | to yourself, scared | Light went three times during orientation. They said it's normal.
:: 2 | to yourself, excited | I found The Spot! I'm never leaving. I live here now.
:: 3 | to yourself | They said sit on the warm bench and you'll graduate. Or not. They argued.
:: 4 | to yourself, amused | First lecture, I sat in the wrong theatre for an hour. Nice course, though.
:: 5 | to yourself, shy | I called a professor 'Mallam'. He said 'Doctor'. I said 'Sorry, Mallam'.
:: 6 | the lights go out, sad, low, during a power cut | Candle is now two hundred? It was one hundred this morning!

::: Common 9 · Year 2: the mall | A shiny new mall has opened, and the lights keep going off.
:: 1 | to yourself, excited | There's a mall now? With escalator? I'm riding it six times.
:: 2 | to yourself | The mall has light all night. The hostel has light 'sometimes'. Hmm.
:: 3 | to yourself, shy | Senior, the shuttle to New Site: is it today, or is it a feeling?
:: 4 | to yourself, amused | I asked how much to charge my phone. He said 'how much do you have?'

::: Common 10 · Year 3: the strike | The lecturers are on strike. No lectures, an empty campus, no money.
:: 1 | to yourself, sad, low, during the strike | I resumed and the lecturers didn't. Is that allowed?
:: 2 | to yourself | My dad said 'go back to school'. Daddy, school is not here.
:: 3 | to yourself, sad, low | Freshers in a strike. We matriculated into a waiting room.
:: 4 | to yourself, amused | I've learned so much this semester. Mostly how to make garri last.

::: Common 11 · Year 4: fame | Viral videos, an election, and work placements.
:: 1 | to yourself, excited | Is it true about the video? Everybody shared it, so it must be true.
:: 2 | to yourself | Which candidate is giving out free biscuits? I'm voting for biscuits.
:: 3 | to yourself, amused | A senior said our hall is haunted. Another said it's just the plumbing.
:: 4 | to yourself, shy | They said the clock stopped before anybody here was born.

::: Common 12 · Year 5: leaving | Final year. Projects, job offers, and everyone counting the days.
:: 1 | to yourself, shy | Senior, you're graduating? Who will show us the shortcuts?
:: 2 | to yourself, excited | The campus looks so bright. My cousin said it was dark before.
:: 3 | to yourself | Is that clock going to start one day, or is it decoration?
:: 4 | to yourself, warm | A finalist gave me her old notes. Full of sweet wrappers. Still a blessing.

::: Common 13 · Special days: term time | Days on the campus calendar. Allowance day is when money from home arrives.
:: 1 | freshers' week, excited | FREE MEAT PIE! Where? WHERE?
:: 2 | freshers' week, scared | The talk said 'look to your left, look to your right'. I looked. Now what?
:: 3 | freshers' week | Admission letter, photocopy, photocopy of the photocopy…
:: 4 | exam week, scared | First exam. I've read the course outline twelve times. Is that reading?
:: 5 | exam week, a whisper | Senior, is it true the invigilator can hear you thinking?
:: 6 | results day, scared | I can't look. You look. Tell me with your face.
:: 7 | results day, excited | I passed! Everything! Even the course I attended once!
:: 8 | allowance day, excited | Allowance came! I have plans. The plans are garri.
:: 9 | market day, amused | Thursday market! I bought a bucket. I don't know why. It was cheap.

::: Common 14 · Special days: the big ones | Save any shouting for last.
:: 1 | the strike, sad, low | Strike? In our first year? We haven't even learned the school song.
:: 2 | cup final day, excited | Which faculty are we? I'm shouting for whoever is winning.
:: 3 | election week, proud | A candidate shook my hand and gave me a pen. I'm loyal now.
:: 4 | graduation day, proud | One day that's me in the gown. Five years. Or six. God willing.

::: Common 15 · Special days: seasons and holidays | Harmattan is the dry, dusty season.
:: 1 | Ramadan, warm | First Ramadan away from home. The iftar here is big. Alhamdulillah. [Praise God.]
:: 2 | Christmas, sad, low | Half the hall travelled for Christmas. The corridor echoes now.
:: 3 | Valentine's Day, scared | One rose is how much? One? Is it made of gold?
:: 4 | harmattan, sad, low | Harmattan has cracked my lips into a map of the campus.
