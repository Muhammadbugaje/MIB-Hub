# Meet Lily

> Whispers to plants. Speaks last. Is right.

A Botany student who lives in the greenhouse, and the quiet twin of the loudest reporter on campus. Very soft, very patient. When she says it will rain, take an umbrella.

### The voice

Early twenties, Botany, the greenhouse; Lyzzy's twin. Very soft, slow and patient; often starts with a pause ("…"). Speaks last and is right. Whispers to plants as if they were people, and to people as if they were plants.

### What is this game?

It's a game you play in a web browser, about surviving five years at a Nigerian university. Please don't expect GTA. Nobody steals a car. The most dangerous vehicle in it is a three-wheeled keke taxi with a married couple arguing in the back, and the nearest thing to a boss fight is a queue.

You play a brand-new student, the Fresher. You register (four offices, four queues, four passport photographs), go broke by week three, live on garri, make friends for life, sit exams by torchlight because the power has gone again, and one day you graduate. Under the jokes there is a mystery: why the lights keep going off, and why a quiet old man has spent forty years in a garden keeping one of them on. Nobody gets hurt. The cure for the monster is company.

The university and the town are made up. The voices are friends and family, recording on their phones. Thank you for being one of them.

# How to record

**What you've got.** **102 lines**, about **930 words**. That's 9 mission lines and 93 common lines. Read straight through, it's **about 10 minutes** of talking. Nobody should do it in one sitting. A few sections a day is perfect.

**Change any word so it sounds like you.** Honestly. If a line doesn't sound right in your mouth, say it your way. If you think of a funnier one, record that as well. You are the expert on how Lily talks.

#### Getting set up

- **A phone is fine.** Use its voice recorder app on the best quality setting, in aeroplane mode so nothing pings.
- **Pick a quiet, soft room.** A wardrobe full of clothes is the best studio in any house. Switch off fans and the TV.
- **Hold the phone about a hand-span from your mouth,** slightly to one side, and keep it there.

#### Reading the page

- **The big black words are your line.** Say only those.
- **The small orange words above a line are how to say it,** and sometimes who you are talking to. Don't read them out.
- **Small grey words are what somebody else says just before you,** so you know the moment. Don't read them out.
- **Words in [square brackets] are the English meaning.** Don't say them. If the Hausa, Pidgin or any other language is wrong, fix it. You know better than I do.
- **(name)** is where the game drops in the player's name: leave a short pause. **Bature/Firdausi** is the player's roommate: say the line once with each name.
- **Three dots (...) mean a beat of silence.**

#### Recording

- **One recording per coloured bar.** Start recording, read that bar's lines in order, stop. Then the next bar.
- **Leave about two seconds of silence between lines.** That's where I cut. No need to say the line numbers.
- **If you fluff a line,** pause and say it again. Don't stop the recording.
- **Got the energy?** Say a line twice, two different ways, and I'll pick the best one.
- **Shouting lines last,** and drink water.

#### Sending it

- Name each file after its coloured bar, like this: *Lily - Mission 1*.
- Put everything in one folder and send me a Google Drive or WeTransfer link, with a photo of the signed last page. There is no deadline.

# Mission lines

In story order. Each coloured bar is one mission: its name, when it happens and what is going on. One recording per bar.

## Common lines

These are the short lines Lily says around campus, outside the story: when the player walks up, bumps into you, hands you a present, or just passes by. The game picks one each time, so they never play in a row. Say each as if someone has just walked past.

::: Common 1 · Saying hello | The player has just walked up.
:: 1 | shy | …Hello. You came the long way. Your shoes say so.
:: 2 | warm | …Oh. Hi. I was just telling the hibiscus about you.
:: 3 | shy | …Good morning. The neem tree says good morning too. Quietly.
:: 4 | warm | …Hi. You look thirsty. So does the bougainvillea. One at a time.

::: Common 2 · Saying goodbye
:: 1 | warm | …Go gently. Take water.
:: 2 | shy | …Bye. Shade on the left side of the road. It's cooler.

::: Common 3 · Thank-yous and sorries
:: 1 | thank you, warm | …Thank you. The plants say so too. They're slower about it.
:: 2 | sorry, shy | …Sorry. I was listening to the leaves. What did you say?

::: Common 4 · Being nice | Compliments. Mean them.
:: 1 | warm | …You're very patient. Like a cactus. That's a compliment.
:: 2 | shy | …You grew this year. Not taller. Better.

::: Common 5 · Teasing | Friendly. The joke is never unkind.
:: 1 | amused | …You overwatered your plant. It told me.
:: 2 | amused | …Lyzzy says you're mysterious. You're just tired.

::: Common 6 · Walking with the player | Keeping up, waiting, and when the player goes the wrong way.
:: 1 | walking along | …I'm coming. Mind the roots.
:: 2 | waiting for the player, shy | …It's all right. I'll wait. Seeds wait for years.
:: 3 | the player went the wrong way | …Not this way. The path by the greenhouse. Follow the tomatoes.

::: Common 7 · Quick answers | The player asks something and gets one of these back.
:: 1 | yes, warm | …Yes.
:: 2 | no, shy | …No. Sorry. No.
:: 3 | maybe | …Maybe. Ask me after it rains.
:: 4 | confused, shy | …Hmm? I was counting leaves. Again, please.
:: 5 | confused, dry, deadpan | …Who? There was nobody there fa. Only the wind.
:: 6 | agreeing, warm | …Mm. Yes. You're right. Lyzzy will say it louder.
:: 7 | refusing | …I'd rather not. Thank you for asking first.

::: Common 8 · Jokes and questions
:: 1 | a joke, amused | …Why was the plant shy? It had a lot of… leaves. Lyzzy, don't laugh.
:: 2 | a question | …Have you had water today? A whole glass?

::: Common 9 · Winning and losing | After a game, a match or a bet.
:: 1 | after winning, warm | …We won. Oh. That's nice.
:: 2 | after losing | …We lost. Things grow back.

::: Common 10 · When something goes wrong | Bumps, falls, power cuts, rain and heat. NEPA is what everybody calls the power company, and so every power cut.

:: 3 | the lights go out, a whisper | …It's dark. The moonflowers will open now. Come and see.
:: 4 | the lights come back, shy | …Light. The moonflowers are cross.
:: 5 | rain, a whisper | …It will rain.
:: 6 | rain, warm | …There. I said.
:: 7 | a hot day | …Too hot. The leaves are folding. So should we. Shade.

::: Common 11 · Food and money
:: 1 | food, warm | …Garden eggs from the greenhouse. I grew them. Don't tell the Dean.
:: 2 | money | …Seeds are cheap. Waiting is free.

::: Common 12 · Getting a present | The player hands over a present. Some go down better than others.
:: 1 | likes the present, warm | …For me? Thank you. I'll put it by the window.
:: 2 | not keen on the present, shy | …Plastic flowers. That's… thoughtful. They won't need me.
