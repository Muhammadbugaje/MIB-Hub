# Voice script: Dr. Zainab

**Cast id:** `npc.zainab` · **Story lines:** 79 · **Barks and filler:** 209 · **Total:** 288

**Voice:** Late twenties; house officer, later junior resident in paediatrics. Warm, quick and funny, clinical when it matters; roasts you while she treats you, goes sharp and quiet when you don't listen. Loves her cats (Kimchi and Suya) and Korean culture; uses a few Korean words correctly. Catchphrases: "Did I stutter?" "Doctor's orders." "Drink water." "Hwaiting!"

**How to record:** read the cue (the line before yours) to get the moment, then say your line in the mood given. Record each line three times, in a quiet room, phone or microphone 20 cm away. Name the files by the line id (for example `m002_open_01.wav`). Words in [brackets] are the translation: do not say them. Every line is a draft: if it does not sound like you, say so, and we change it.
