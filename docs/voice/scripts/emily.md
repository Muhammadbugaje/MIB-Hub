# Meet Emily

> Answers all bad news with one word: "Strawberry?"

A Crop Science student whose family grows strawberries beyond New Site. Always running, always slightly out of breath, always cheerful, and unable to walk past a sad person without feeding them.

### The voice

Early twenties, Crop Science; her family's strawberry farm beyond New Site. Always a little out of breath, cheerful, rushed. Answers any bad news with "Strawberry?" and gives her stock away to anyone who looks sad.

### What is this game?

It's a game you play in a web browser, about surviving five years at a Nigerian university. Please don't expect GTA. Nobody steals a car. The most dangerous vehicle in it is a three-wheeled keke taxi with a married couple arguing in the back, and the nearest thing to a boss fight is a queue.

You play a brand-new student, the Fresher. You register (four offices, four queues, four passport photographs), go broke by week three, live on garri, make friends for life, sit exams by torchlight because the power has gone again, and one day you graduate. Under the jokes there is a mystery: why the lights keep going off, and why a quiet old man has spent forty years in a garden keeping one of them on. Nobody gets hurt. The cure for the monster is company.

The university and the town are made up. The voices are friends and family, recording on their phones. Thank you for being one of them.

# How to record

**What you've got.** **107 lines**, about **1,108 words**. That's 12 mission lines and 95 common lines. Read straight through, it's **about 15 minutes** of talking. Nobody should do it in one sitting. A few sections a day is perfect.

**Change any word so it sounds like you.** Honestly. If a line doesn't sound right in your mouth, say it your way. If you think of a funnier one, record that as well. You are the expert on how Emily talks.

#### Getting set up

- **A phone is fine.** Use its voice recorder app on the best quality setting, in aeroplane mode so nothing pings.
- **Pick a quiet, soft room.** A wardrobe full of clothes is the best studio in any house. Switch off fans and the TV.
- **Hold the phone about a hand-span from your mouth,** slightly to one side, and keep it there.

#### Reading the page

- **The big black words are your line.** Say only those.
- **The small orange words above a line are how to say it,** and sometimes who you are talking to. Don't read them out.
- **Small grey words are what somebody else says just before you,** so you know the moment. Don't read them out.
- **Words in [square brackets] are the English meaning.** Don't say them. If the Hausa, Pidgin or any other language is wrong, fix it. You know better than I do.
- **(name)** is where the game drops in the player's name: leave a short pause. **Bature/Firdausi** is the player's roommate: say the line once with each name.
- **Three dots (...) mean a beat of silence.**

#### Recording

- **One recording per coloured bar.** Start recording, read that bar's lines in order, stop. Then the next bar.
- **Leave about two seconds of silence between lines.** That's where I cut. No need to say the line numbers.
- **If you fluff a line,** pause and say it again. Don't stop the recording.
- **Got the energy?** Say a line twice, two different ways, and I'll pick the best one.
- **Shouting lines last,** and drink water.

#### Sending it

- Name each file after its coloured bar, like this: *Emily - Mission 1*.
- Put everything in one folder and send me a Google Drive or WeTransfer link, with a photo of the signed last page. There is no deadline.

# Mission lines

In story order. Each coloured bar is one mission: its name, when it happens and what is going on. One recording per bar.

## Common lines

These are the short lines Emily says around campus, outside the story: when the player walks up, bumps into you, hands you a present, or just passes by. The game picks one each time, so they never play in a row. Say each as if someone has just walked past.

::: Common 1 · Saying hello | The player has just walked up.
:: 1 | excited | Hi! Sorry! I ran! I always run! Strawberry?
:: 2 | warm | Morning! I've been up since five. The cows don't do lie-ins.
:: 3 | excited | Oh, hello! Hold this. No, the basket. Thank you. Hi!
:: 4 | warm | Sannu! [Hello!] You look sad. Here. Strawberry.

::: Common 2 · Saying goodbye
:: 1 | excited | Bye! I have to run. The tractor is waiting. Tractors don't wait.
:: 2 | warm | Go! Eat something red today. Doctor's orders. I'm not a doctor.

::: Common 3 · Thank-yous and sorries
:: 1 | thank you, warm | Thank you! Here, take two. No, three. Okay, the basket.
:: 2 | sorry, excited | Sorry! Sorry! My basket hit you. It's heavy. It's love.

::: Common 4 · Being nice | Compliments. Mean them.
:: 1 | warm | You look so healthy! Like a strawberry in the right season.
:: 2 | excited | You can carry a sack! Can you also drive a tractor? No? Soon!

::: Common 5 · Teasing | Friendly. The joke is never unkind.
:: 1 | amused | You've never seen a farm before? Look. That's a cow. It's looking at you.
:: 2 | amused | You call that running? I run with a basket on my head.

::: Common 6 · Walking with the player | Keeping up, waiting, and when the player goes the wrong way.
:: 1 | walking along, excited | Come, come! Faster! The shuttle leaves at four, and it means four-ish!
:: 2 | waiting for the player, excited | Faster! The cows have been waiting since six! So have I!
:: 3 | waiting for the player, excited | Hurry! The strawberries are ripening while we stand here!
:: 4 | the player went the wrong way | That's the road to Agriculture. Keep going past the cows. The cows know.

::: Common 7 · Quick answers | The player asks something and gets one of these back.
:: 1 | yes, excited | Yes! Yes! Strawberry?
:: 2 | no, warm | No. But here. Strawberry.
:: 3 | maybe | Maybe. Ask me after harvest.
:: 4 | confused, excited | Wait, what? Say it slower. I'm breathing.
:: 5 | confused, dry, deadpan | Who? There was nobody there fa. Just Senator. He's a ram.
:: 6 | agreeing, excited | Exactly! That's what I told the Dean! He said 'later'!
:: 7 | refusing, warm | No, I can't. But take this strawberry so you're not sad about it.

::: Common 8 · Jokes and questions
:: 1 | a joke, amused | Why did the strawberry cry? It was in a jam! Sorry. My father's joke.
:: 2 | a question, warm | Have you eaten? You haven't. Strawberry. Then go to Mama Nkechi.

::: Common 9 · Winning and losing | After a game, a match or a bet.
:: 1 | after winning, excited | We won! Strawberries for everyone! Within reason!
:: 2 | after losing, warm | We lost. Strawberry?
:: 3 | after losing, warm | Strawberry? It doesn't fix it. It helps.

::: Common 10 · When something goes wrong | Bumps, falls, power cuts, rain and heat. NEPA is what everybody calls the power company, and so every power cut.
:: 1 | bumped into, excited | Oh! Sorry! Was that the basket or me? The basket. Probably.
:: 2 | after a fall, amused | I'm fine! I've fallen off worse. Like a tractor.
:: 3 | the lights go out | NEPA again. At the farm we don't notice. We go to bed with the chickens.
:: 4 | the lights come back, excited | Light! Quick, the cold room! The strawberries!
:: 5 | rain, excited | Rain! Finally! The farm is dancing! I'm dancing!
:: 6 | a hot day | Too hot. Strawberries hate this. So do I. Shade.

::: Common 11 · Food and money
:: 1 | food, warm | Mama Nkechi gives me rice. I give her strawberries. Best trade on campus.
:: 2 | money, dry, deadpan | I made forty naira profit this week. I gave away the rest. Again.

::: Common 12 · Getting a present | The player hands over a present. Some go down better than others.
:: 1 | likes the present, excited | For me? Oh, thank you! I'll put it on the tractor! For luck!
:: 2 | not keen on the present, warm | Oh. Thank you. I'll… give it to someone sad. That's what I do.
:: 3 | loves the present, excited | Seeds! Proper seeds! You remembered! I'm going to cry on them!

::: Common 13 · Talking to yourself | Said to nobody in particular as the player walks past.
:: 1 | excited | Basket. Phone. Notes. Basket. Where's my other basket?
:: 2 | warm | Senator! Get back here! Sorry. That's the ram. He's having a day.

::: Common 14 · Late at night | After dark. Quieter.
:: 1 | a whisper | At night the fields are so quiet. You hear everything. Even strawberries.
