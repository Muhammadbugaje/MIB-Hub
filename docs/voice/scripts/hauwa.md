# Meet Nurse Hauwa

> Doctor is coming. Sit.

The night nurse at the teaching hospital. She has seen everything twice and was not impressed either time. Flat, calm and far kinder than she sounds.

### The voice

A northern night nurse in her forties at the TH. Flat, unshockable, quietly kind. Has seen everything twice. "Doctor is coming. Sit."

### What is this game?

It's a game you play in a web browser, about surviving five years at a Nigerian university. Please don't expect GTA. Nobody steals a car. The most dangerous vehicle in it is a three-wheeled keke taxi with a married couple arguing in the back, and the nearest thing to a boss fight is a queue.

You play a brand-new student, the Fresher. You register (four offices, four queues, four passport photographs), go broke by week three, live on garri, make friends for life, sit exams by torchlight because the power has gone again, and one day you graduate. Under the jokes there is a mystery: why the lights keep going off, and why a quiet old man has spent forty years in a garden keeping one of them on. Nobody gets hurt. The cure for the monster is company.

The university and the town are made up. The voices are friends and family, recording on their phones. Thank you for being one of them.

# How to record

**What you've got.** **65 lines**, about **524 words**. That's 18 mission lines and 47 common lines. Read straight through, it's **about 7 minutes** of talking. Nobody should do it in one sitting. A few sections a day is perfect.

**Change any word so it sounds like you.** Honestly. If a line doesn't sound right in your mouth, say it your way. If you think of a funnier one, record that as well. You are the expert on how Nurse Hauwa talks.

#### Getting set up

- **A phone is fine.** Use its voice recorder app on the best quality setting, in aeroplane mode so nothing pings.
- **Pick a quiet, soft room.** A wardrobe full of clothes is the best studio in any house. Switch off fans and the TV.
- **Hold the phone about a hand-span from your mouth,** slightly to one side, and keep it there.

#### Reading the page

- **The big black words are your line.** Say only those.
- **The small orange words above a line are how to say it,** and sometimes who you are talking to. Don't read them out.
- **Small grey words are what somebody else says just before you,** so you know the moment. Don't read them out.
- **Words in [square brackets] are the English meaning.** Don't say them. If the Hausa, Pidgin or any other language is wrong, fix it. You know better than I do.
- **(name)** is where the game drops in the player's name: leave a short pause. **Bature/Firdausi** is the player's roommate: say the line once with each name.
- **Three dots (...) mean a beat of silence.**

#### Recording

- **One recording per coloured bar.** Start recording, read that bar's lines in order, stop. Then the next bar.
- **Leave about two seconds of silence between lines.** That's where I cut. No need to say the line numbers.
- **If you fluff a line,** pause and say it again. Don't stop the recording.
- **Got the energy?** Say a line twice, two different ways, and I'll pick the best one.
- **Shouting lines last,** and drink water.

#### Sending it

- Name each file after its coloured bar, like this: *Nurse Hauwa - Mission 1*.
- Put everything in one folder and send me a Google Drive or WeTransfer link, with a photo of the signed last page. There is no deadline.

# Common lines

These are the short lines Nurse Hauwa says around campus, outside the story: when the player walks up, bumps into you, hands you a present, or just passes by. The game picks one each time, so they never play in a row. Say each as if someone has just walked past.

::: Common 1 · Saying hello | The player has just walked up.
:: 1 | bored, flat | Doctor is coming. Sit.
:: 2 | your normal voice | Sannu. [Hello.] What happened? Short version.
:: 3 | dry, deadpan | You again. Is it the foot or the friend this time?
:: 4 | bored, flat | Name. Hall. What did you eat. In that order.

::: Common 2 · Saying goodbye
:: 1 | firm | Go. Drink water. Not soft drink. Water.
:: 2 | your normal voice | Sai da safe. [Until morning.] Don't come back tonight.

::: Common 3 · Thank-yous and sorries
:: 1 | thank you, bored, flat | Thank me by sleeping before two.
:: 2 | sorry | Sorry is fine. Sit is better.

::: Common 4 · Teasing | Friendly. The joke is never unkind.
:: 1 | dry, deadpan | Third visit this month. I'm giving you your own chair.
:: 2 | bored, flat | 'It's just a headache.' Said every finalist at 3 a.m.

::: Common 5 · Walking with the player | Keeping up, waiting, and when the player goes the wrong way.
:: 1 | bored, flat | Doctor is coming. Doctor is always coming. Sit.
:: 2 | dry, deadpan | You are standing. Why are you standing? Sit.

::: Common 6 · Quick answers | The player asks something and gets one of these back.
:: 1 | yes, bored, flat | Yes. Sit.
:: 2 | no, firm | No. You cannot see Doctor first. Nobody sees Doctor first.
:: 3 | confused, dry, deadpan | Say it again. Slower. I have heard stranger. Last Tuesday.

::: Common 7 · Jokes and questions
:: 1 | firm | When did you last eat? Garri doesn't count. Fine, it counts.
:: 2 | your normal voice | How many hours did you sleep? Don't round up.

::: Common 8 · When something goes wrong | Bumps, falls, power cuts, rain and heat. NEPA is what everybody calls the power company, and so every power cut.
:: 1 | bumped into, bored, flat | You fell. Get up. I'll look at it. It's nothing. Sit.
:: 2 | after a fall, dry, deadpan | Ah-ah. In front of a nurse. Brave.
:: 3 | the lights go out, bored, flat | Light gone. Generator in thirty seconds. Nobody move.
:: 4 | the lights come back | There. Light. Now I can see what you did to yourself.

::: Common 9 · Food and money
:: 1 | firm | Eat. Then talk. Then Doctor.

::: Common 10 · Getting a present | The player hands over a present. Some go down better than others.
:: 1 | likes the present, warm | Kunu. Still warm. Fine. You can stand for one minute.
:: 2 | not keen on the present, dry, deadpan | Energy drink. I'll put it with the others. In the bin.
:: 3 | loves the present, warm | A good torch. For the long corridor. …Thank you. Sit.

::: Common 11 · Talking to yourself | Said to nobody in particular as the player walks past.
:: 1 | bored, flat | Bed four is wheezing. Bed four is always wheezing.
:: 2 | dry, deadpan | Doctor has eaten. Mark the calendar.

::: Common 12 · Late at night | After dark. Quieter.
:: 1 | a whisper | Quiet tonight. Don't say it out loud. It hears.
:: 2 | your normal voice | Night shift. The tea is old and so am I.

::: Common 13 · Year 1: arriving | Everything is new. Queues, friends, the first power cuts.
:: 1 | looking back, dry, deadpan, after “Doctor's Orders” | The fresher with the goat bite. I remember. Sit.
:: 2 | to yourself, bored, flat | First-years. Malaria or heartbreak. Same queue.

::: Common 14 · Year 2: the mall | A shiny new mall has opened, and the lights keep going off.
:: 1 | to yourself | Night shift again. Like eggs: run, and you spill.
:: 2 | being nice, dry, deadpan | You carried that well. Nothing spilled. Good.

::: Common 15 · Year 3: the strike | The lecturers are on strike. No lectures, an empty campus, no money.
:: 1 | the strike | Strike? Not for us. Sickness doesn't strike.
:: 2 | the strike, a whisper | Snow White, eat first. The queue will keep.
:: 3 | to yourself, dry, deadpan | My day off. And yet. Here I am. Sit them down.

::: Common 16 · Year 4: fame | Viral videos, an election, and work placements.
:: 1 | to yourself, firm | Keep it noisy. Not loud. Noisy. People heal near noise.
:: 2 | to yourself, bored, flat | Somebody filmed in my ward. I have confiscated the ring light.

::: Common 17 · Year 5: leaving | Final year. Projects, job offers, and everyone counting the days.
:: 1 | to yourself, firm | That is my seat. I don't care what you are.
:: 2 | to yourself, warm | Final year. You still come by. Good. Sit. Just sit.

::: Common 18 · Special days: term time | Days on the campus calendar. Allowance day is when money from home arrives.
:: 1 | freshers' week, bored, flat | Freshers' week. Blisters from walking to the wrong theatre.
:: 2 | exam week, firm | Exam week. Panic attacks on the left. Sit.
:: 3 | exam week, dry, deadpan | Coffee is not blood. Go and sleep.

::: Common 19 · Special days: the big ones | Save any shouting for last.
:: 1 | cup final day, dry, deadpan | Football Saturday. I have prepared ice. And patience.

::: Common 20 · Special days: seasons and holidays | Harmattan is the dry, dusty season.
:: 1 | Ramadan | Fasting and fainting? Sit. Dates are in my drawer.
:: 2 | Christmas, warm | Half the campus has travelled. The other half fell off something.
:: 3 | harmattan, bored, flat | Harmattan. Cracked lips, catarrh. Vaseline is on the table.
