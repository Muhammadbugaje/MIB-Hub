# Voice script: DJ Kabiru

**Cast id:** `npc.kabiru` · **Story lines:** 14 · **Barks and filler:** 45 · **Total:** 59

**Voice:** Ring Road FM station head, late forties, a voice like warm bread: low, slow, smiling. Talks off air the way he talks on air. "You're on the Ring Road."

**How to record:** read the cue (the line before yours) to get the moment, then say your line in the mood given. Record each line three times, in a quiet room, phone or microphone 20 cm away. Name the files by the line id (for example `m002_open_01.wav`). Words in [brackets] are the translation: do not say them. Every line is a draft: if it does not sound like you, say so, and we change it.
