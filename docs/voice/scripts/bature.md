# Voice script: Bature

**Cast id:** `npc.roomie`, `npc.bature` · **Story lines:** 141 · **Barks and filler:** 246 · **Total:** 387

**Voice:** Male, about twenty; Mass Communication, later presenter of "Riddles After Dark" on Ring Road FM. These lines are only his own: the radio, his course, and lines for when he is not your roommate (a female player meets him as Firdausi's cousin). His real radio voice is warm and good; off air he is the clown.

**How to record:** read the cue (the line before yours) to get the moment, then say your line in the mood given. Record each line three times, in a quiet room, phone or microphone 20 cm away. Name the files by the line id (for example `m002_open_01.wav`). Words in [brackets] are the translation: do not say them. Every line is a draft: if it does not sound like you, say so, and we change it.
